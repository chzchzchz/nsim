Starts or stops the heater drain pump, which moves condensate from the heater drain tank into the feed train. With it off, drains simply accumulate in the tank until high level starts spilling back to the condenser and the feed pumps lose their deaerator suction inventory.

The pump is what turns the drain tank from a sump into the deaerator: "the tanks are cross-connected by a 20-in. line" and drained continuously so fourth-stage heater drains keep flowing downstream instead of flooding. Watch heater drain tank level against pump state - a running pump with rising level means suction trouble, not recovery.
