Steam temperature leaving the moisture separator reheater on its way to the low-pressure turbine stages. The MSR squeezes out moisture from HP exhaust then reheats the dry steam, boosting LP stage efficiency and limiting blade erosion.

Falling outlet temperature with steady load suggests reheat side trouble - expect higher moisture content downstream and watch for vibration or erosion indications.
