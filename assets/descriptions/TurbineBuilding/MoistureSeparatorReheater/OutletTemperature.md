Steam temperature leaving the moisture separator reheater on its way to the low-pressure turbine stages. HP turbine exhaust is wet - maybe 10-14 percent moisture - and passing that through LP blading erodes leading edges and wastes energy; the MSR first mechanically separates the water, then reheats the dry steam with live and reheat steam.

Falling outlet temperature with steady load suggests reheat-side trouble: expect higher moisture content downstream, declining stage efficiency, and eventually vibration or erosion indications. Outlet temperature holding near design is quiet evidence the LP stages will survive their service life.
