Runs the condenser vacuum pump. The condenser works by keeping a sub-atmospheric steam space - turbine exhaust condenses on cooled tubes and the resulting collapse in specific volume is what gives a steam plant most of its efficiency; air leaking in destroys that by blanketing tube surfaces and raising backpressure.

Under normal operation air is removed continuously by steam jet air ejectors or vacuum pumps like this one. Starting vacuum is a prerequisite for rolling the turbine; deteriorating vacuum with the pump healthy points at air in-leakage or circulating water problems - check [condenser pressure](TurbineBuilding/Condenser/Pressure) trend against [CW flow](Outside/CirculatingWaterSystem/Flow).
