Runs the condenser vacuum pump. Air removal establishes and maintains the sub-atmospheric pressure the turbine exhaust needs - poor vacuum directly costs output and raises heat rate.

Under normal operation air is removed by steam jet air ejectors or vacuum pumps; the condenser must also absorb steam dump flow during transients with only acceptable backpressure increase.
