Engages turning gear, rotating the shaft slowly (a few rpm) during shutdown periods so the rotor cannot sag and bow under its own weight unevenly, and after trips so cooling happens symmetrically rather than with hot metal pooled at the bottom of the casing.

Sequence discipline matters: lube oil circulation first, then turning gear, and gear continues until rolling speed replaces it or the rotor is cold. Starting rotation without oil, or stopping rotation on a cooling rotor, converts a routine shutdown into an overhaul.
