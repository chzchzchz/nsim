Starts the auxiliary lube oil pump supplying bearing oil. Bearing oil pressure must exist before shaft rotation and persist through coast-down after any trip - the coast-down can take tens of minutes at full speed.

Low bearing oil pressure is a turbine trip condition in its own right; watch [bearing oil pressure](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/BearingOilPressure) whenever pump state changes.
