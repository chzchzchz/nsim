Starts the auxiliary lube oil pump supplying bearing oil. Every journal and thrust bearing depends on a pressurized oil film measured in thousandths of an inch; the pump's job is to make that film exist before the shaft turns and keep it present through the tens of minutes a full-speed coast-down takes after any trip.

Low bearing oil pressure is itself a turbine trip condition, and the standard response to any oil-system suspicion - degraded pressure, AC pump trouble, pre-start checkout - is starting the auxiliary pump early. Watch [bearing oil pressure](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/BearingOilPressure) whenever pump state changes.
