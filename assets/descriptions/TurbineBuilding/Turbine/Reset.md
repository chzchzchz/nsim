Clears the turbine trip latch once trip conditions have cleared, re-arming the electro-hydraulic control so the stop valve can be reopened and load restored.

Follow turbine-generator instructions when returning: establish vacuum and lube oil, reset, open stop valve, roll on speed demand, synchronize, then reload. Resetting into an existing fault simply retrips.
