Clears the turbine trip latch once trip conditions have cleared, re-arming the electro-hydraulic control so the stop valve can be reopened and load restored.

Follow turbine-generator instructions when returning: establish condenser vacuum, confirm lube oil pressure, reset the latch, open the stop valve, roll the shaft on [speed demand](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/SpeedDemand) through critical speeds, synchronize via the generator breaker, then reload. Resetting into an existing fault simply retrips - the latch clearing proves permissives were met at that instant, nothing more.
