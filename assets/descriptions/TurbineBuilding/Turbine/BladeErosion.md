Cumulative low-pressure turbine blade erosion from steam moisture carryover, as a percent of one damage unit. Wet steam at low load carries liquid past the moisture separator reheaters; above the removal allowance the carryover chews the last-stage blades at a rate proportional to the excess. At sustained low-load carryover the full scale is reached in about 700 hours, so this is a slow wear ledger, not a trip.

Read it with the turbine load and the [OutletTemperature](TurbineBuilding/MoistureSeparatorReheater/OutletTemperature): prolonged low-load operation in a wet steam cycle is what pays this fraction down. It feeds the turbine part damage readout, so a machine that has run wet for weeks carries permanent efficiency loss even after the carryover is fixed.

Model note: the erosion rate and the carryover allowance are simulator-derived; the reference names the moisture-carryover mechanism but gives no rate.
