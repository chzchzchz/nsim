Governor speed demand as a fraction of rated speed - the rolling handle used before synchronization. Raise it gradually to accelerate the shaft through its critical speeds (resonances where rotor imbalance amplifies vibration) toward 1800 rpm nominal.

Once frequency matches the grid, closing the generator breaker locks the machine electrically; after that the same governor hardware translates speed-error signals into load sharing per droop, which is how every unit on a grid absorbs random ±5 percent load swings without anyone touching setpoints. Rolling without adequate lube oil or turning-gear warmup risks journal damage before the fun even starts.
