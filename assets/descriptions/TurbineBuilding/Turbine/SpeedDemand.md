Governor speed demand as a fraction of rated speed - the rolling handle used before synchronization. Raise it gradually to accelerate the shaft through its critical speeds toward 1800 rpm nominal.

Once frequency matches the grid, close the generator breaker; after synchronization the same governor hardware translates speed-error signals into load sharing per droop. Rolling without adequate lube oil or turning-gear warmup risks rotor damage.
