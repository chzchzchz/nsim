Indicates extraction line non-return (check) valves are closed. Extraction steam feeds feedwater heaters off the turbine; the NRVs prevent water or steam from flowing backward into the machine.

They slam shut on turbine trip or load rejection - reverse flow protection that has saved many trains of blading from water-induced damage.
