Indicates extraction line non-return (check) valves are closed. Extraction steam bleeds off the turbine intermediate stages to feed feedwater heaters; the NRVs exist so reverse flow - water or steam pushing backward from the heaters toward the machine - cannot happen.

They slam shut on turbine trip or large load rejection. The scenario they prevent is ugly: a heater at higher pressure dumping back through a stalled turbine adds torque and sometimes water to blading designed for one-way flow. Closed NRVs after a trip are correct operation, not a fault.
