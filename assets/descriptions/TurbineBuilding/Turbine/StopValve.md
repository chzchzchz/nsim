Turbine stop (trip) valve. Fully open it admits main steam to the high-pressure turbine; snapping shut isolates steam admission in a fraction of a second and is the mechanical heart of every turbine trip - overspeed, low bearing oil, trip pushbutton, magnetic detection of water in steam all end here.

After a trip the valve stays latched shut until the turbine is [reset](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/Reset) and conditions permit restart. The steam that cannot enter the turbine has to go somewhere: dump valves to the condenser or atmospheric relief take it, because reactor power cannot follow instantly. A stop valve that closes on-load while rods are slow to react is precisely the >10 percent load rejection scenario the steam dump system exists for.
