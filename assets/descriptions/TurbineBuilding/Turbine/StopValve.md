Turbine stop (trip) valve. Fully open it admits main steam to the high-pressure turbine; snapping shut isolates steam admission in well under a second and is the mechanical heart of every turbine trip.

After a trip the valve stays latched shut until the turbine is reset and conditions permit restart. Steam that cannot enter the turbine must go somewhere - steam dump to the condenser or [atmospheric dump](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/AtmosphericDumpValve/Open) takes it, which is why trip response couples the whole secondary together.
