Opens the turbine bypass dump path, admitting main steam directly to the condenser without passing through the turbine. This decouples reactor power from electrical demand during transients.

The dump valves open automatically under sudden load reductions above 10 percent or on turbine trip, because nuclear power cannot be instantly reduced and the steam must be rejected somewhere. They also serve in pressure-control mode during startup and cooldown, letting reactor power come up to about 10 percent before synchronization by dumping to the condenser.
