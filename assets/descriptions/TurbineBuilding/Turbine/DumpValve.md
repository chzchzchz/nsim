Opens the turbine bypass dump path, admitting main steam directly to the condenser without passing through the turbine - the pressure-relief joint between a nuclear core that cannot change quickly and an electrical demand that can change instantly.

The valves fire open automatically under sudden load reductions exceeding 10 percent or on turbine trip, rejecting the steam the reactor is still making until rods bring power down. They also run in pressure-control mode during startup and cooldown, letting the operator raise reactor power to about 10 percent before synchronization by dumping to the condenser, establishing minimum turbine load without a grid disturbance. The condenser must be able to absorb all this - its design includes surface specifically for dump flow at acceptable backpressure increase.
