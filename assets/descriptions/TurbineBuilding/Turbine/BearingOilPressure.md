Bearing lubricating oil pressure in psig. Every journal and thrust bearing depends on it; sustained low pressure damages rotors within moments and typically trips the turbine on low-low.

Keep an auxiliary lube oil pump ready via [LubeOilPump](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/LubeOilPump) whenever main oil supply is suspect, especially through trips and coast-downs.
