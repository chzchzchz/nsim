Bearing lubricating oil pressure in psig. The entire rotating assembly - hundreds of tons spinning at 1800 rpm - rides on oil films thinner than a human hair; pressure is the only evidence those films exist.

Low pressure threatens metal-to-metal contact within moments and typically trips the turbine on low-low setpoint before damage completes. The standard discipline: verify auxiliary pump availability ([LubeOilPump](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/LubeOilPump)) before any planned oil system evolution, and treat unexplained pressure drift as a prelude to trip, not noise.
