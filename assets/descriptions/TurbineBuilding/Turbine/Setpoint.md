Turbine load setpoint as a fraction of rated capacity - the target the digital electro-hydraulic controller drives governing valve position toward.

The DEHC computes control signals comparing speed and first-stage pressure with reference values from the dispatcher or operator panel, then positions the governing and intercept valves through electro-hydraulic actuators. Changing setpoint while unsynchronized affects speed; online it changes load directly.
