Turbine load setpoint as a fraction of rated capacity - the target the digital electro-hydraulic controller drives governing valve position toward.

The DEHC computes control signals comparing turbine speed and first-stage pressure with reference values established by the dispatcher or initiated at the operator's panel, then positions the governing and intercept valves through high-pressure hydraulic actuators. While rolling (unsynchronized), changing setpoint changes shaft acceleration; once online, it changes electrical load almost directly, with first-stage pressure following within seconds.
