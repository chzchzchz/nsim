Mechanical power the turbine delivers to the generator shaft. Compare against generator electrical output to see generator losses; large divergence with steady steam conditions suggests measurement or coupling trouble.

Steam flow through the HP and LP stages sets this number - first-stage pressure tracks it almost linearly, which is why first-stage pressure doubles as a load signal inside the DEHC.
