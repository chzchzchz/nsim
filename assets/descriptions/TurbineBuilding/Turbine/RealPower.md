Mechanical power the turbine delivers to the generator shaft. At steady state this equals electrical output plus generator losses; transient mismatches between the two accelerate or decelerate the rotor until governor action rebalances steam flow.

Steam flow sets the number directly, which is why first-stage pressure - proportional to flow through the governing valves - doubles as a fast load signal inside the DEHC control computations. Watching mechanical power against generator output is also a cheap consistency check: divergence with steady steam means measurement or coupling trouble upstream of the electrical side.
