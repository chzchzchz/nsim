Turbine shaft speed in rpm, nominally 1800 at rated conditions for this four-pole design - the number that becomes exactly 60 Hz electricity once synchronized.

While rolling, speed is the primary controlled variable, accelerated on demand and held steady through critical speed ranges. After synchronizing it stops being interesting (locked to the grid); after a trip it becomes the coast-down curve - the rate of decay reveals bearing condition and windage losses to anyone who knows what normal looks like.
