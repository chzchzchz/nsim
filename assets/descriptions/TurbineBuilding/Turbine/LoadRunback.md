Forces a controlled reduction of turbine load - runback instead of trip - when equipment limits say full output cannot continue, for example loss of a feedwater pump or condensate constraint.

A managed runback keeps the unit online and lets rod control follow demand down, versus the abrupt >10 percent step that would fire the steam dump system and walk Tavg off program.
