Forces a controlled reduction of turbine load - runback instead of trip - when equipment limits say full output cannot continue: loss of one main feedwater pump, condensate constraint, condenser trouble short of vacuum loss.

A managed runback keeps the unit online and lets rod control follow demand down the temperature program smoothly. The alternative is an abrupt step greater than 10 percent, which fires the steam dump system, walks Tavg off its program, and asks the protection systems whether this was actually a trip instead. Runback logic is therefore a plant-friendliness feature: same destination, far gentler road.
