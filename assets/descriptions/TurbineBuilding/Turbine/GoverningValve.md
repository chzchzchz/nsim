Governing (control) valve position from closed (0) to fully open (1). These valves throttle HP steam to control either shaft speed while rolling or electrical load once online, actuated by the electro-hydraulic system responding to DEHC commands.

Together with the reheat intercept valves they form the entire forward path of turbine control; the microprocessor controller computes their demanded position from speed and first-stage pressure feedback against reference values. Hunting (cycling position) shows up immediately as load wobble; sluggish response shows up as droop during dispatch changes.
