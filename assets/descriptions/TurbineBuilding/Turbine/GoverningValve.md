Governing (control) valve position from closed (0) to fully open (1). These valves throttle HP steam to control either speed while rolling or load once online, actuated by the electro-hydraulic system from DEHC commands.

Together with intercept valves they form the entire forward path of turbine control; hunting or sluggish position here shows up immediately as load or frequency wobble.
