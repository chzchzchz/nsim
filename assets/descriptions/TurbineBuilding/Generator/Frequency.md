Electrical frequency at the generator terminals in Hz - exactly 60 once synchronized, since the magnetic lock to the grid forces it. Before synchronization it is shaft speed expressed electrically, matched against the grid as part of the sync permissives alongside voltage magnitude and phase.

Online, small deviations are the grid breathing: every unit absorbs them via droop without setpoint changes. Sustained deviation means system-level trouble - islanding, major loss of generation - where your unit's governor response becomes part of the solution or part of the problem.
