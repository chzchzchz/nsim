Real electrical power delivered to the grid by the main generator in watts - the endpoint of the entire fission-to-socket chain this simulator models, and the number the dispatcher actually pays for.

Steady-state bookkeeping says turbine mechanical power minus electrical and friction losses equals this value; transient imbalance accelerates or decelerates the rotor until governor action rebalances steam. Real power responds to governor valve position; reactive power (not shown separately here) responds to excitation instead - confusing the two control handles is the classic young operator mistake.
