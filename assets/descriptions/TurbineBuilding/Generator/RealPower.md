Real electrical power delivered to the grid by the main generator. This is the number the dispatcher cares about and the endpoint of the entire heat cycle.

Steady-state balance means turbine mechanical power minus electrical losses equals this value; transient mismatches accelerate the rotor until governor action rebalances steam flow.
