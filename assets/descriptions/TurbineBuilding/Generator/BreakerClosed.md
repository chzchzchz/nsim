Closes the main generator output breaker onto the switchyard, synchronizing the unit to the grid. Before closing, generator voltage and frequency must be matched to [grid voltage](ReactorBuilding/Grid/Voltage) and [frequency](ReactorBuilding/Grid/Frequency) - the synchroscope check.

After synchronization, minimum load of 5-10 percent is established partly by dumping steam to the condenser so reactor power has somewhere to go; then load follows dispatcher demand through the governor.

See also: [Voltage](ReactorBuilding/Grid/Voltage), [Frequency](ReactorBuilding/Grid/Frequency), [SpeedDemand](TurbineBuilding/Turbine/SpeedDemand), [LoadDemand](ReactorBuilding/Grid/LoadDemand)
