Generator terminal voltage, held near nominal by the excitation system. The brushless static excitation with field flashing gives rapid voltage buildup for quick synchronization after startup.

Voltage control is reactive power control in practice: raising excitation exports VArs, lowering imports them. Push too far either way and capability limits start annunciating.
