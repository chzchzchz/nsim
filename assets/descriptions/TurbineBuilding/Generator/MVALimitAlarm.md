Annunciates that the generator is pressing against its MVA capability limit - almost always from reactive load rather than raw megawatts. The capability curve bounds real plus reactive output; high VArs at depressed voltage or absorbed VArs at high output walk the operating point toward stator or rotor thermal edges.

Sustained operation beyond the curve overheats windings - damage measured in months of derated life per hour of abuse. Standard response: adjust excitation to trim VAr exchange, or trim real load via the turbine until the operating point comes inside the curve again.
