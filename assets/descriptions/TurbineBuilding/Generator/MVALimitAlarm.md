Annunciates that the generator is pressing against its MVA capability limit - typically from high reactive load at elevated or depressed terminal voltage rather than raw megawatts.

Sustained operation beyond capability curves overheats stator windings or rotor. Response is usually reducing VAr exchange via excitation adjustment or trimming real load until inside the curve again.
