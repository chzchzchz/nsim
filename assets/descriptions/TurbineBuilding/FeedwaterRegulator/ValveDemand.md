Manual demand for the feedwater regulating valve, from shut (0) to fully open (1). Under automatic steam generator level control the controller owns this valve; taking manual demand hands SG level to the operator directly.

Automatic level control is one of the three automatics the reference startup sequence enables (rod control, SG level, steam dump Tavg mode) precisely because hand-matching feedflow to steam demand across load changes is continuous work: every megawatt moved changes steam off-take, and level responds with the swell/shrink lag in between. Manual mode remains the right place during startup heatup when flows are tiny and slow, and during level control troubleshooting.
