Manual demand for the feedwater regulating valve, from shut (0) to fully open (1). Under automatic level control the controller owns this valve; taking manual demand hands SG level control to the operator.

During normal operation the steam generator level control system maintains inventory through these valves automatically; manual regulation is mainly a startup and troubleshooting tool.
