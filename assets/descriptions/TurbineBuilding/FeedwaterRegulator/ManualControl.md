Transfers SG level control between the three-element automatic regulator and manual valve demand. In manual, the regulator holds your valve demand instead of integrating level, steam flow, and feedwater flow errors.

The transfer itself is procedural furniture: "reactor control and steam generator water level control are transferred to manual operation" at defined plant evolutions, and the unload sequence performs it with the unit at house load. Manual level control is a two-hands task - feedwater changes move SG level with swell/shrink lag, so every adjustment needs a pause-and-watch before the next one. Go back to auto before power maneuvers; three-element control reacts to load steps far faster than hand trimming.
