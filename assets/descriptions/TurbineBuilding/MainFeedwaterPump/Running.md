Runs the main feedwater pump returning heated feedwater to the steam generators under normal operation. The reference design drives these with small turbines fed by reheated steam.

Trip or loss of the running MFPs is a classic runback driver: reactor power must fall to what remaining feed capacity can cool, or emergency feedwater takes over if none can.
