Runs the main feedwater pump returning heated feedwater to the steam generators under normal operation - the biggest pump job in the secondary cycle, pushing full-load feed mass flow against SG pressure. The reference design drives these with small turbines fed by reheated steam rather than electric motors.

Trip of running MFPs is the classic runback driver: reactor power must fall quickly to what remaining feed capacity can cool. If no feed path survives at all, [emergency feedwater](AuxiliaryBuilding/EmergencyFeedwaterSystem/SubsystemA/MotorPumpRunning) takes over the decay heat removal role - designed for exactly that handoff, sized to hold hot standby indefinitely.
