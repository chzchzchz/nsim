Runs the condensate pump moving hotwell water forward into the feedwater heating train. Losing it drains the hotwell down and starves everything downstream - deaerator, booster, and main feed pumps.

Condensate pumps sit at the bottom of the secondary cycle's inventory loop; their suction conditions depend on condenser vacuum and hotwell level, both worth glancing at when pump behavior looks odd.
