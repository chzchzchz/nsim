Runs the condensate pump moving hotwell water forward into the feedwater heating train - the first mechanical lift out of the condenser's deep vacuum, which is why these are low-suction-head machines living at the very bottom of the secondary cycle.

Losing condensate flow drains the hotwell down and starves everything downstream: deaerator storage, feedwater booster suction, eventually the main feed pumps themselves. Condensate also carries the chemistry program - oxygen scavengers and pH control enter here - so pump trouble is a chemistry event as well as an inventory one.
