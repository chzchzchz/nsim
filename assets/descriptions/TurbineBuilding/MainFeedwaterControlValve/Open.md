Main feedwater control valve position from shut (0) to fully open (1) - the primary throttle for normal feedwater into the SGs. The automatic level control system positions it to hold programmed level.

On loss of normal feedwater these valves fail toward their safety alignment while [emergency feedwater](AuxiliaryBuilding/EmergencyFeedwaterSystem/SubsystemA/MotorPumpRunning) picks up the heat removal role.
