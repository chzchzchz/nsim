Main feedwater control valve position from shut (0) to fully open (1) - the primary throttle for normal feedwater into each steam generator. The automatic level control system positions it continuously to hold programmed level as steam demand moves.

On loss of normal feedwater these valves fail toward their safety alignment while the [emergency feedwater system](AuxiliaryBuilding/EmergencyFeedwaterSystem/SubsystemA/MotorPumpRunning) picks up heat removal through separate, always-open discharge paths - the design principle being that EFW needs no valve lineup to deliver its first gallon within the minute the analysis credits.
