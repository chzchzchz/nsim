Engages automatic gray (control) rod control. While latched, the controller integrates the error between measured Tavg and the programmed reference temperature into an insertion demand each step, replacing the manual demand input.

The rod control system supports both manual and automatic operation of the rod groups. In manual mode insertion or withdrawal runs at a preset fixed rate chosen by the operator; in automatic the rod speed varies with the size of the temperature error, moving fast for large deviations and creeping near the target. Group sequencing follows a preset reversible program - withdrawal order is the reverse of insertion order.
