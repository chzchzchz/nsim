Simulator construct with no plant control: a training malfunction filed with the control-system faults that impairs the plant control system's power reduction. With this latched, a requested stepback cannot execute; the reference frames the shutdown system as the backstop: "However, the reactor shutdown system (SDS) is always poised to act, should those alarm limits reach the trip setpoint."

Stepback is normally the first line of defense - an automatic runback of demanded power that catches process excursions before they become trips. With it failed, watch how the same disturbance resolves: what used to be caught by a graceful power cut now runs on toward the trip channels. The reference frames the malfunction around a turbine trip ("due to the malfunction, the reactor stepback cannot be initiated; therefore Control Rods will not respond to turbine trip"); the point is to exercise the protection chain behind the control system.
