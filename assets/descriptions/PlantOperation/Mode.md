Selects the plant operating mode: **ColdShutdown**, **HotStandby**, or **PowerOperation**. Mode placement is an operator action taken during startups and shutdowns rather than something the control system does on its own.

A cold startup begins with precritical checks and lineup: electrical power available to everything needed, instrumentation calibrated, and fluid systems valved in as required. Above 200F primary temperature, Technical Specifications compliance is verified before continuing heatup - containment integrity secured and auxiliary feedwater operable are two explicit examples. Hot standby means the plant is subcritical at no-load temperature and pressure with steam dump holding Tavg, ready to resume power operation.
