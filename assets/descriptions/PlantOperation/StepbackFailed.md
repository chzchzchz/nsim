Training malfunction that impairs the plant control system's power reduction: with this latched, a requested stepback cannot execute, and the reactor shutdown system becomes "the only remaining protection" for the transient.

Stepback is normally the first line of defense - an automatic runback of demanded power that catches process excursions before they become trips. With it failed, watch how the same disturbance resolves: what used to be caught by a graceful power cut now runs on toward the trip channels. The malfunction is a way to exercise the protection chain behind the control system, per the training reference's own framing.
