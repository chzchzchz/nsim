Programmed reference average coolant temperature (Tref) for the present power level. Automatic rod control drives measured Tavg to this value, and steam generator level programs and steam dump references hang off the same signal.

During normal operation the rod control system holds RCS average temperature as a programmed function of turbine load, so Tref rises smoothly from roughly 560F at hot standby to about 600F+ at full power in a typical program. After large load decreases the steam dump system augments rod control specifically to bring Tavg back to program without a large temperature transient.
