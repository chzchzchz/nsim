The dispatch permissive: your grant that lets remote dispatch demands reach the turbine load reference. Off, the unit holds whatever power you have set regardless of what dispatch asks; on, the grid dispatcher's setpoint ramps the machine.

It exists because the network must filter what reaches the turbine - "to act as a permissive network so that unsafe demands from the computer" never drive the plant directly. Granting it during normal operation puts the unit in ordinary commercial service; granting it while maneuvering or with a degraded parameter doubles every disturbance the grid sends you.
