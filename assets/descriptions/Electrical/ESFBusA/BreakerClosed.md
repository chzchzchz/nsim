Feeder breaker for engineered safety features bus A. A source - off-site transformer or diesel generator - can only deliver voltage to the train with this breaker closed, so it gates every downstream load pickup.

The power sources feeding ESF buses are physically and electrically independent up to the point of connection, which is why each bus has its own breaker logic and its own [diesel](AuxiliaryBuilding/DieselGenerator/StartSignal).
