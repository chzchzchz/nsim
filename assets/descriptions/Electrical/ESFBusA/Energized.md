Live indication for ESF bus A. Normally energized from the system auxiliary transformers off the grid; on loss of offsite power the associated diesel picks the bus up automatically.

Critical safety buses are deliberately excluded from routine transfers between auxiliary transformer sources - their supply continuity outranks economic switching.
