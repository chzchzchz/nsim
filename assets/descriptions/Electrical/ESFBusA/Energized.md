Live indication for ESF bus A. Normally energized from the system auxiliary transformer fed by the off-site grid; on loss of offsite power the associated [diesel](AuxiliaryBuilding/DieselGenerator/StartSignal) picks the bus up automatically within seconds.

Critical safety buses are deliberately excluded from routine transfers between auxiliary sources during plant maneuvering - their supply continuity outranks economic switching. An de-energized ESF bus with no diesel running is among the most urgent conditions a control room can face.
