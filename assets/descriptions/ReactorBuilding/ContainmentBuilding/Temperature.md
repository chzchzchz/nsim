Containment atmosphere temperature in Fahrenheit. It tracks the same events as [containment pressure](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentPressure) with a thermal lag: mass and energy releases raise both, condensation on structures and spray droplets lowers both, but the huge concrete and steel thermal mass slows temperature relative to the snappier pressure signal.

Post-accident, containment fan coolers and spray work together to return the atmosphere to acceptable bands; the reference design requires a minimum of two of four fan units operating for both normal and post-accident conditions. Temperature matters beyond comfort - equipment inside containment must survive it, and iodine scrubbing effectiveness degrades as conditions worsen.
