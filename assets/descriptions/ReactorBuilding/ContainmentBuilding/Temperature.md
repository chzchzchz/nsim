Containment atmosphere temperature in Fahrenheit. Steam released into containment condenses on structures and spray droplets, so temperature tracks the same events that drive [containment pressure](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentPressure) with a thermal lag.

Post-accident, fan coolers and spray work together to return the atmosphere to acceptable bands; the reference design requires a minimum of two of four fan units for both normal and post-accident operation.
