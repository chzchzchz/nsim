Starts containment atmosphere circulation. The purpose is mixing: "a containment ventilation system that circulates the containment atmosphere to remove pockets of high hydrogen concentration after an accident." Hydrogen rises and stratifies; circulation keeps it dispersed so no single volume reaches a dangerous concentration ahead of the bulk.

After actuation confirm flow on [VentilationRunning](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/HydrogenDetectionSuppression/VentilationRunning) and watch [HydrogenPercent](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/HydrogenDetectionSuppression/HydrogenPercent). Circulation distributes the problem, recombination removes it - the two run together.
