Acknowledgement pushbutton for the high-hydrogen annunciator. The alarm condition "turned off by operator acknowledgement" - the model requires exactly that: the alarm holds until someone presses this, and only once suppression has restored margin.

Acknowledging is bookkeeping, not a corrective action: it silences the horn but does nothing to [HydrogenPercent](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/HydrogenDetectionSuppression/HydrogenPercent). The corrective actions are circulation ([Ventilation](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/HydrogenDetectionSuppression/Ventilation)) and recombination. Acknowledge so the next excursion stands out, not instead of acting.
