Status of the hydrogen recombiners, which control hydrogen by chemistry: "Hydrogen recombiners that burn the free hydrogen. The hydrogen/oxygen combination results in water." Every percent of hydrogen consumed leaves the containment as condensate instead of flammable gas.

Running recombiners show up here while [HydrogenPercent](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/HydrogenDetectionSuppression/HydrogenPercent) bends downward. They are the removal half of the strategy; [Ventilation](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/HydrogenDetectionSuppression/Ventilation) is the distribution half that keeps any one pocket from concentrating first. If concentration climbs with the recombiner shown running, capacity is being outrun by generation - a sign of significant uncovered-fuel metal-water reaction in severe scenarios.
