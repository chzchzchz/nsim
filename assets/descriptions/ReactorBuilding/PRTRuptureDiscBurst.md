Indicates the rupture disc on the pressurizer relief tank has burst. The disc is the tank's own overpressure protection: a deliberately weak membrane that fails at design pressure and routes everything further to open containment. Bursting is one-way - discs do not reseat.

After a burst, continued PORV or safety-valve discharge stops being contained: steam flows into containment, driving up [containment pressure](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentPressure) and [temperature](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/Temperature), and engaging the same plant responses as any other mass-and-energy release inside containment. Recovery requires plant shutdown of relief sources plus replacing the disc.
