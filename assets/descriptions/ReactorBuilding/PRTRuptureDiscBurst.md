Indicates the rupture disc on the pressurizer relief tank has burst. The disc is the tank's final overpressure protection: once it ruptures, relief steam vents straight into containment instead of being condensed in the tank.

Bursting is one-way - the disc does not reseat. Containment pressure and temperature will climb with continued discharge, engaging the same responses as any other mass-and-energy release inside containment.
