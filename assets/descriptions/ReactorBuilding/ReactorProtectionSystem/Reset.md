Resets the RPS trip latch and the ESFAS output latches after an actuation. Select Reset from the dropdown and apply.

Reset cannot defeat an active condition: while trip channels still satisfy 2/4 coincidence voting, the step function re-declares the trip immediately. Return to service after a genuine trip proceeds from hot standby with rods withdrawn back to criticality per the startup sequence.
