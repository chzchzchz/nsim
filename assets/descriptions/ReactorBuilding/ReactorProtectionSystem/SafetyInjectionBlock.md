Operator block on the safety injection initiation signals, armed before a planned cooldown depressurizes toward the automatic actuation setpoints. Without the block, pressurizing down through the SI setpoint would spuriously start injection.

Prior to reaching the automatic SI setpoints during cooldown and depressurization, the SI initiation signals are blocked - a standard lineup step in the shutdown sequence, paired with isolating the [accumulators](ReactorBuilding/SafetyInjectionSystem/AccumulatorIsolation/Shut).
