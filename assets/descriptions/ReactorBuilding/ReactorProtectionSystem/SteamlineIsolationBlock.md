Blocks the automatic steam-line isolation signal. Use it to keep routine cooldowns from tripping isolation on low steamline pressure - the setpoint that protects against a main steam break also sits close to ordinary cooldown pressures.

"Block steamline isolation below a preset pressure" is the standard companion to the isolation function itself (chapter 16 trip response): during planned plant cooldown the operator arms the block so depressurization does not slam MSIVs shut. The discipline is symmetric - verify the block is removed before power operation, because with it in you have traded away the fast closure that limits a steam-line break's blowdown.
