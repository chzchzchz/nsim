Operator-initiated safety injection. One deliberate action that drives the same safeguards sequence an automatic SI signal would: injection start, Phase A containment isolation, and the emergency feedwater and support-system lineups that follow.

The automatic circuits watch low pressurizer pressure and other ESFAS functions; this switch exists for the cases they cannot see - confirmed small breaks with pressure decaying too slowly, or instrument uncertainty where waiting is the riskier choice. "Manual initiation of safety injection" sits in the protection system chapter because it is a protection action, not a convenience start: expect [SI actuated](rps.si_actuated) annunciation, charging/HHSI realignment, and the plant stepping into its post-accident lineup.
