Sprays cold water into the pressurizer relief tank. The tank holds enough water to condense steam arriving from the relief and safety valves; spraying increases its heat sink capacity when sustained relief flow threatens to overpressurize it.

A rupture disc on the tank vents to containment if design pressure is exceeded anyway - watch [rupture disc status](ReactorBuilding/PRTRuptureDiscBurst) whenever PORV or safety flow is running.
