Sprays cold water into the pressurizer relief tank. The tank normally holds enough water at near-atmospheric conditions to condense steam arriving from the relief and safety valves; spraying adds heat sink capacity when sustained relief flow threatens to fill or overheat it.

If tank pressure rises past design anyway, a rupture disc vents the whole tank to containment - watch [rupture disc status](ReactorBuilding/PRTRuptureDiscBurst) whenever PORV or safety valve flow is running. After any significant relief event the tank must be refilled, cooled, and its disc integrity confirmed before it can accept another transient.
