Off-site grid frequency in Hz, nominal 60. The generator can only synchronize when its frequency matches the grid within limits - closing a breaker across mismatched frequencies slams torque through the rotor and can bend shafts.

Once online the unit supports grid frequency inherently through governor droop: every unit on the grid sees the same frequency, and random load variations of about ±5 percent are absorbed by governor action rather than rod motion, which minimizes control rod wear and disturbance to the axial power distribution. Sustained under-frequency means the grid is overloaded and dispatchers will want maximum available output; over-frequency suggests light load.
