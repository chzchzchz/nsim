Off-site grid frequency in Hz, nominal 60. The generator can only synchronize when its frequency matches the grid within limits, and once online the unit inherently supports grid frequency through governor droop.

Grid frequency deviations matter beyond synchronization: sustained under-frequency indicates a heavily loaded grid where the dispatcher may call for maximum output; over-frequency suggests light load and possible runback demand.
