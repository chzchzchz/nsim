Dispatcher load demand on the unit as a fraction of rated electrical output. The turbine load reference latches this value each step, and the governor moves the governing valves toward it.

The reference design compares turbine speed and first-stage pressure against reference values established by the dispatcher or initiated at the operator's panel; the remote dispatch interface guards the boundary by transmitting plant power capability upward but refusing unsafe dispatch demands.
