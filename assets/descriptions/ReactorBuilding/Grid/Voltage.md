Off-site grid voltage. Before closing the generator breaker the operator matches generator voltage to grid voltage - the excitation system raises terminal voltage until the synchroscope shows acceptable difference.

Off-site power is also the normal source for the ESF buses via the system auxiliary transformers, so grid conditions couple directly into the electrical lineup described under the ESF bus entries.
