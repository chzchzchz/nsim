Off-site grid voltage. Before closing the generator breaker the operator matches generator terminal voltage to grid voltage - the excitation system raises or lowers field current until the synchroscope shows acceptable difference in magnitude as well as frequency and phase.

Off-site power has a second identity that matters more during accidents: it is the normal source feeding the ESF buses through the system auxiliary transformers. Grid trouble therefore couples into safety systems directly - loss of offsite power starts the diesels, and degraded voltage on these lines is itself a protective trip concern.
