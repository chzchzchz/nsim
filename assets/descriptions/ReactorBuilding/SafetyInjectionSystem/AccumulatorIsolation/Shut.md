Closes the SI accumulator isolation valves. Accumulators are passive tanks: no pumps, no signals, no power - just a large volume of borated water pressurized by nitrogen over about 600 psig, connected to the cold legs through check valves. Whenever RCS pressure drops below the nitrogen precharge, the check valves open and the tank dumps its contents into the reactor in seconds, covering the gap before pumps can inject.

That passivity is why the valves normally stand open so the tanks stay discharge-ready at all times. During a planned cooldown, though, RCS pressure will deliberately pass through the discharge band, so the operator shuts the isolation valves first - part of the same lineup as blocking the [SI signal](ReactorBuilding/ReactorProtectionSystem/SafetyInjectionBlock).
