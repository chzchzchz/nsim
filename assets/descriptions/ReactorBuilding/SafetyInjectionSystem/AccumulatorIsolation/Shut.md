Closes the SI accumulator isolation valves. The accumulators are passive tanks that discharge automatically whenever RCS pressure falls below their nitrogen precharge, so during a planned cooldown the operator shuts their isolation valves before pressure reaches the discharge point.

Normally the valves stand open so the tanks stay discharge-ready at all times; closing them is part of the same planned-cooldown lineup as blocking the SI signal.
