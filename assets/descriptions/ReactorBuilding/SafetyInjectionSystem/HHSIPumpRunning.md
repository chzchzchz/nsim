Manually starts the high-head safety injection (HHSI) pump - "high-head" because its shutoff pressure exceeds normal RCS pressure, letting it inject into a pressurized primary. From the main control board, manual starting backs up the CVCS charging pumps for RCS boration; as RCS pressure falls below the pump's shutoff head, real injection of borated water into the cold legs begins.

In an accident the same pump starts automatically on the SI signal and runs on miniflow - a small recirculation line that protects the pump from dead-heading while RCS pressure is still too high to inject against. When pressure falls below shutoff head, injection begins, first drawing from the refueling water storage tank and later realigned to the containment sump for long-term recirculation after the RWST is exhausted.
