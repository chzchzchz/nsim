Realignment for simultaneous injection into both the RCS cold legs and hot legs, performed manually from the control board: "Approximately 24 hours after the switchover to cold-leg recirculation, simultaneous flow to the RCS cold legs and hot legs is established."

The timing matters. Cold-leg-only injection is right for the early recirculation phase; about a day in, boron and steam start accumulating where cold-leg flow does not reach, so part of the flow is diverted to the hot legs for long-term core cooling and boron-buildup prevention. Setting this starts the split - watch it show up in [HHSIHotLegFlow](ReactorBuilding/SafetyInjectionSystem/HHSIHotLegFlow).
