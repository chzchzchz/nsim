The pressurizer pressure target, in psia, that automatic heater and spray regulation holds. It anchors the whole primary pressure program: nominal operation sits here, low pressure eats shutdown margin toward the SI setpoint, high pressure walks toward the PORV and safety valves.

Change it rarely and deliberately. Raising it moves every overpressure limit closer; lowering it erodes the subcooling margin that keeps the core covered against a small loss-of-coolant transient. The automatic machinery around it - [heaters](ReactorBuilding/Pressurizer/HeaterAutoControl) below the band, [spray](ReactorBuilding/Pressurizer/SprayOpen) above - does the minute-to-minute work.

Model note: stored and displayed in psia; control-room practice quotes psig.
