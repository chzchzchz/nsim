Pressurizer steam pressure in psig - effectively RCS pressure. Full-power operation sits near 2250 psia with heaters and spray holding it against insurges and outsurges in accordance with the applicable temperature-pressure curves.

Watch it together with [pressurizer level](ReactorBuilding/PressurizedReliefTank/Level): level is programmed against Tavg, so a sudden level swing with steady temperature usually means a pressure transient is underway. Sustained high pressure walks up to the PORV setpoint, then the [code safety valves](ReactorBuilding/SafetyValve/Open).
