Opens the pressurizer spray valve, admitting relatively cool reactor coolant from a cold leg into the steam space where it condenses bubbles and lowers pressure. Spray is the fast, controllable downward pressure actuator.

The pressure control system uses spray whenever insurge transients - load rejection, plant heatup - push pressure above band. If spray alone cannot hold the line during a large load reduction, the [PORV](ReactorBuilding/PORV/Open) opens automatically below design pressure as the next stage of protection.
