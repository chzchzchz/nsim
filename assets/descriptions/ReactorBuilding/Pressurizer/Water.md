Water inventory in the pressurizer in gallons. Level is programmed as a function of Tavg: charging flow from the CVCS is adjusted automatically to follow the program, typically held between 20 and 80 percent of range.

Too little level uncovers the heaters and exhausts the surge cushion; too much leaves no room for an insurge and floods spray effectiveness. During startup the operator raises letdown flow specifically to bring level down into the normal band before heatup.
