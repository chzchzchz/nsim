Energizes the pressurizer immersion heaters. During a cold startup all heaters are switched on to begin the pressurizer heatup needed to form the steam bubble; without the bubble the RCS has no compressible cushion and pressure control is impossible.

Once the bubble exists, heaters become a fine pressure-control tool: water flashing to steam limits pressure reductions, and energizing the heaters boils more steam to limit them further. Pressure is maintained by balanced use of heaters and [spray](ReactorBuilding/Pressurizer/SprayOpen).
