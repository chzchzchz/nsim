State of the off-site supply to the safeguards buses. Available means the independent, electrically isolated grid feeds are standing behind both ESF buses; lost, it is the LOOP that auto-starts both diesels and begins sequenced loading of the safe-shutdown loads.

"On loss of normal electrical power to a safeguards electrical bus, backup emergency diesel generators are automatically started and necessary equipment will be automatically sequenced on the buses." The flag is the model's name for that supply state: "the system auxiliary power is supplied from independent and electrically isolated buses of the off-site main distribution grid." Watch [ESF bus](Electrical/ESFBusA/Energized) transfer timing when it drops, and remember restoration re-energizing dead buses is a deliberate, checked action.
