Arms the reactor makeup control valve to admit makeup water automatically when primary inventory calls for it - the seal-table and letdown losses during normal operation are exactly what this path replaces without operator action.

The valve sits on the chemical-and-volume control side feeding the RCS: "Because of the influx of makeup water from the reactor coolant..." its flow is ordinary operation, not an emergency function. In auto it opens on inventory demand; take it to manual only for deliberate draindown or fill exercises where automatic response would fight your plan, and watch [Initiated](ReactorBuilding/ReactorMakeupControlValve/Initiated) to see the demand signal the sequence runs on.
