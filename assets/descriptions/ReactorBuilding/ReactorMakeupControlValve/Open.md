Opens the reactor makeup control valve, admitting makeup water from the chemical and volume control system into the RCS. This is how the simulator models influx of makeup water during inventory additions.

Use it together with the CVCS controls: charging pump state and [boron mode](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/BoronMode) determine what kind of water arrives, while this valve meters how much.

Related controls: [BoronMode](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/BoronMode)
Related sensors: [ChargingFlow](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/ChargingFlow)
