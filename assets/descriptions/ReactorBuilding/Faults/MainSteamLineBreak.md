Simulator construct with no plant control: a normalized opening size upstream of the MSIVs filed with the accident faults. Nothing on any control board can sever the main steam header; 1 inserts the full "100 percent main steam header break" from the pwr_sim malfunction catalog.

An uncontrolled secondary-side opening dumps steam faster than the reactor can follow: SG pressure and temperature collapse, and the cold primary side sees an aggressive overcooling transient - positive reactivity from the cooldown, rising power, and SI-level responses on the way down. The MSIVs are the countermove; closing isolation caps how much of the header keeps feeding the break. Watch [Pressure](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/Pressure) for the collapse rate and the trip channels it drives.
