Dissolved boric acid concentration in the reactor coolant in ppm - the slow chemical shim complementing the control rods. Boron is a strong neutron absorber dissolved directly in the primary water; operational reactivity control is provided jointly by the rods and this soluble poison, with rods handling seconds-scale changes and boron handling hours-scale ones.

Raising concentration adds negative reactivity: cooldown margin, shutdown assurance, compensating for fresh fuel. Dilution removes it: approaching criticality, countering burnup over a fuel cycle, trimming out excess margin. During startup from hot standby the operator calculates the boron concentration that puts criticality at a chosen rod position - higher boron means rods must come out further, and the [insertion limit](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/ControlRods/Insertion) bounds how far is acceptable - then starts a dilution to reach it.
