Dissolved boric acid concentration in the reactor coolant in ppm - the slow chemical shim complementing the control rods. Operational reactivity control is provided jointly by the rods and soluble boron.

Raising concentration adds negative reactivity (cooldown margin, shutdown); dilution removes it (approaching criticality, countering burnup and xenon). During startup from hot standby a calculated dilution brings boron to the estimated critical value so rods can be withdrawn to a position above the insertion limit.
