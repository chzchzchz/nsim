Cumulative clad oxidation as a percent of the 17 percent acceptance cap (10 CFR 50.46 limit b). Above 1472 F steam reacts with zirconium by the Baker-Just parabolic law, and the reaction enthalpy itself heats the clad further. The fraction advances only while the core is uncovered and hot, and it stops at the cap even though physical oxidation continues past it.

It is the second 50.46 criterion read in percent: 100 percent here is exactly at the limit. Paired with [CladTemp](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/CladTemp) it shows how much margin the temperature excursion has consumed; the hydrogen generated by this reaction is what fills [ContainmentAirActivity](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentBuilding/ContainmentAirActivity) in a severe accident. The reaction rate scales with the square root of time above 1472 F, so long slow steam exposure burns nearly as much as a short spike to the same temperature.
