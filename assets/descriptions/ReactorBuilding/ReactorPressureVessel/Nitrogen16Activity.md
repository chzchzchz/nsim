N-16 gamma activity in the hot leg, as a percent of rated fission equilibrium. N-16 is a short-lived (7.13 second half-life) activation product that dies quickly once coolant leaves the core. Because the level is directly proportional to fission rate, the hot-leg reading divided by the core-exit temperature rise gives coolant flow: the basis of the transit-time flow measurement.

Its short half-life makes it a fast power follower: steps in this reading lag [ExcorePowerRange](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/ExcorePowerRange) by seconds, not hours. After a trip the reading collapses within a minute, while xenon and iodine inventories linger for hours, which is why N-16 is the prompt power witness.

Model note: the 7.13 second half-life is an external standard value; the reference states proportionality to fission rate but no half-life.
