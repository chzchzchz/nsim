Cumulative thermal energy produced by the core since the start of the simulation, in megawatt-hours thermal. This is the time integral of total thermal power, including fission power, fission-product decay heat, and reactor coolant pump heat input.

It is the energy-side counterpart of fuel burnup, defined as the amount of energy produced per unit weight of depleted fuel (MWD/MTU). Divided by core uranium mass, this total gives average core burnup. Decay heat continues to add to the total after a trip until the fission-product inventories fully decay.

Sim instrumentation, not a spec requirement: the reference defines burnup as a concept but specifies no cumulative counter.
