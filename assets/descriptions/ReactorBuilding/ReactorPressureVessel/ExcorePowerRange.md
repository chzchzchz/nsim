Excore power-range detector sum in percent of full power, from the four-section chamber. It reads zero below 25 percent power, then tracks p times 100 up to 120 at p equals 1.20, the 120 percent full-power ceiling of the power range. Same percent-of-full-power convention as the intermediate range, so the handover at p equals 0.25 is continuous at 25.

It is the operating power meter above a quarter power: rod control, turbine load following, and reactivity maneuvers are all judged on it. Saturation above 120 percent means the detector is out of range, not that the reactor stopped at 120. Check [ExcoreIntermediateRange](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/ExcoreIntermediateRange) in the overlap window for a second opinion.

Model note: the 25 percent lower edge and the continuous handover are simulator-derived; the reference gives only the 120 percent ceiling.
