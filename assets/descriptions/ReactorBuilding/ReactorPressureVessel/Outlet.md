Reactor coolant outlet temperature (hot leg) in Fahrenheit, nominal design value 558F (292C) at full power. The hot leg carries core-heated water from the vessel to the steam generators, so it responds directly to the balance of core power versus coolant flow.

Tavg - the average of hot and cold leg temperatures across loops - is the controlled variable the rod system tracks against its program. A hot leg excursion above program with a steady cold leg points at excess power or lost flow; both legs rising together is a plant-wide heatup, typically a feedwater or steam-side problem rather than a reactivity one.
