Cumulative gallons discharged through the hot-leg break that injection credit cannot retire, in gallons. Relief discharge, tube-rupture leakage, cavity drain, and mid-loop drain all feed non-creditable losses, so this floor holds up even when long-term recirculation is refilling the vessel. It is the part of the break loss the plant cannot win back.

Read it with [BreakDischarged](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/BreakDischarged) to see how much of the total loss is permanently banked. A large non-creditable floor with full injection running means the low [Uncovery](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/Uncovery) reading comes from credit, not from intact inventory, and further relief or tube-rupture flow keeps raising the floor even after injection has arrested the break term. Like the total it tracks, it is one-way and never drains back.
