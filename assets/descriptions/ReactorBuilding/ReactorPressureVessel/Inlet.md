Reactor coolant inlet temperature (cold leg) in Fahrenheit, nominal design value 621F (327C) at full power. The cold leg is loop piping returning from the steam generator to the reactor vessel; because the core has not yet heated it, it reflects the heat removal happening in the SGs more than core power.

The gap between inlet and [outlet](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/Outlet) is core heating divided by coolant flow - the classic Delta-T power meter. Inlet rising while outlet holds steady means heat removal is degrading (SG fouling, lost feedwater, closing isolation); both rising together means power is up or flow is down. Cold leg temperature also feeds the Tc comparison that drives boration and dilution decisions.
