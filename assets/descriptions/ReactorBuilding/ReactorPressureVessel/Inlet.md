Reactor coolant inlet temperature (cold leg) in Fahrenheit, nominal design value 621F (327C) at full power. Cold leg temperature is essentially unaffected by core heating - it reflects loop return conditions set by the SGs.

The gap between inlet here and [outlet](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/Outlet) is core heating divided by flow: watch both to read power-flow balance at a glance.
