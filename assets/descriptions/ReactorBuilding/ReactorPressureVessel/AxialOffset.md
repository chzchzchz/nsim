Axial power distribution offset in percent: top-half core power minus bottom-half, over total. Zero means the flux centroid sits at midplane; positive is top-heavy, negative bottom-heavy.

Rod insertion pushes it negative (rods enter from the top and damp the upper half); xenon-135 swings drag it back over hours because the poison burns off where flux concentrates. "An additional concern during power operation is the axial power distribution ... xenon oscillations" - the offset is exactly that oscillation made visible. Keep it inside the operating band during maneuvers: a large offset with high total power loads the peak-to-average ratio toward fuel limits even when average power looks ordinary.
