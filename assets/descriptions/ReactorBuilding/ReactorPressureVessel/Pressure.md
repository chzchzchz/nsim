Reactor coolant system pressure in psig, full-power operating point 2250 psia with design pressure 2500 psia. The pressurizer gas cushion is what keeps this number steady through transients.

Protection stages above normal control band run spray, then PORV, then code safeties; below band, low pressure feeds the SI actuation logic. Pressure plus temperature together define whether RHR may be connected and whether the vessel is in its brittle-fracture concern region during cooldown.
