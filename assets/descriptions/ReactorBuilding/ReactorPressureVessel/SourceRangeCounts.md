Source-range count rate in counts per second from the four proportional-counter channels set just outside the core. Neutron flux produces current pulses in the detectors; the pulse rate tracks population, so a floored plant reads the standing neutron-source level and each decade of chain growth adds a decade of counts.

Rising source range counts with no rod motion and a positive [StartupRate](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/StartupRate) is the criticality evidence during a dilution startup; an audible count-rate signal covers the same window in the control room.

Model note: count rate scales fission power linearly with the neutron-source floor mapped to 1000 cps; the reference names the channels and their behavior but gives no counts-per-flux calibration.
