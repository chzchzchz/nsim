Hottest-assembly core temperature rise in Fahrenheit: bulk outlet minus inlet, inflated by the derived 10 percent peaking factor. At full load the reference rise is 63 F (621 F inlet, 558 F outlet), so the hottest assembly reads about 69 F. This is the delta-T the departure-from-nucleate-boiling ratio (DNBR) margin and the core-exit thermocouple (CET) overtemperature protection watch.

A wide delta-T with a steady mean means the peaking is growing, usually an axial skew or a flow starvation in one loop. Compare it against [Inlet](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/Inlet) and [Outlet](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/Outlet) to separate mean heating from distribution skew.

Model note: the 10 percent peaking factor is derived; the reference gives the 63 F reference rise but no hottest-assembly peaking.
