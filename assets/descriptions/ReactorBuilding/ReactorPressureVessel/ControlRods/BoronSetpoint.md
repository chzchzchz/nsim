Target RCS boron concentration in ppm handed to the CVCS boration/dilution logic. The operating mode and the magnitude of the required change are determined by comparing measured cold leg temperature with the programmed Tc-for-power value: too cold relative to program means excess reactivity (borate), too warm means deficit (dilute).

This setpoint is the operator-visible handle on the slow shim path. Fast transients belong to the rods, which move in seconds; chemistry moves in minutes to hours as charging pumps trade letdown for boric-acid-bearing or pure water. The full path runs through [CVCS mode](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/BoronMode), the boron thermal regeneration beds, and the reactor water makeup system.
