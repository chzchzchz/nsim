Control rod insertion demand from fully withdrawn (0) to fully inserted (1). This is the operator's manual reactivity handle; actual rod motion is rate-limited by the drive speed so the core never sees a step change.

After trip-reset, rods are manually withdrawn to achieve criticality. Keep in mind the rod insertion limit: rods must be pulled high enough that a subsequent trip inserts enough negative reactivity, which is why boron dilution rather than deep rod withdrawal provides startup reactivity.
