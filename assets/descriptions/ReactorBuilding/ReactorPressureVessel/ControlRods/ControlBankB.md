Control bank B position indication (0 fully withdrawn, 1 fully inserted). The four control banks withdraw in sequence A through D with about half a bank span of overlap - bank B starts moving while A still travels - so the combined differential worth stays nearly linear and reactivity additions stay predictable during the manual approach to criticality.

Operators watch the sequence and the overlap on these indications while nuclear instrumentation guards the rate; criticality is reached at a selected bank position above the rod insertion limit.

Model note: the bridge exposes one aggregate rod demand; this reading maps it onto the overlapped bank program for visibility.
