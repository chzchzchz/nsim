Lumped noble-gas coolant activity in the primary, in normalized units where 1.0 is rated-power equilibrium. Production tracks fission power; removal is radioactive decay with the Xe-133 half-life plus entrainment in coolant that leaves the vessel. It is the inventory that the waste gas system strips through the hydrogen carrier and routes to charcoal delay.

A rising reading well above the power-proportional equilibrium points at a clad defect or at a loss pathway feeding outside: compare against [IodineActivity](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/IodineActivity) to see whether both classes are up (fission-driven) or only noble gas is up (leak-driven). It feeds the containment air and vent pathways in a LOCA, which is why reflood before a large break release matters. The lumped primary inventory is a model simplification.
