Manual reactor trip pushbutton. Tripping opens the rod drive trip breakers, which de-energizes the magnetic gripper coils holding each rod cluster control assembly; the grippers release and the rods fall into the core by gravity in about two seconds. No motors, no operator timing - the design is deliberately fail-safe so any loss of power inserts negative reactivity.

The protection system also trips automatically on its engineered signals (low pressure, high flux, low SG level and the rest of the RPS trip set), but the manual pushbutton exists because redundant control board pushbuttons are provided for operator-initiated trip - the classic "the operator sees something the logic does not" escape hatch.

After a trip, decay heat (initially several percent of full power, falling slowly) still needs removal via steam dump or atmospheric relief. Return to service runs from hot standby: reset the [protection latches](ReactorBuilding/ReactorProtectionSystem/Reset), then withdraw [rods](ReactorBuilding/ReactorPressureVessel/ControlRods/Insertion) back toward criticality.
