Manual reactor trip pushbutton. Dropping the trip opens the rod drive trip breakers, de-energizing the gripper coils and letting the rod cluster control assemblies fall into the core by gravity - the classic fail-safe "trip and dump" actuation.

The protection system also trips automatically on its engineered signals; the manual pushbutton exists because redundant control board pushbuttons are provided for operator-initiated trip. After a trip, return to service runs from hot standby: rods withdrawn back toward criticality once [RPS reset](ReactorBuilding/ReactorProtectionSystem/Reset) has cleared the latches.
