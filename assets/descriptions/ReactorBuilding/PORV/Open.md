Power-operated relief valve on the pressurizer steam space. It opens automatically at a setpoint below system design pressure when load-rejection pressure rises exceed what [spray](ReactorBuilding/Pressurizer/SprayOpen) can absorb, and it can be opened manually from the control room console.

Discharge goes to the [pressurizer relief tank](ReactorBuilding/PressurizedReliefTank/Level), not containment. A stuck-open PORV is a classic small LOCA precursor, which is why position indication matters as much as command.
