Power-operated relief valve on the pressurizer steam space - the first powered line of defense against overpressure. It opens automatically at a setpoint below system design pressure whenever load-rejection insurges exceed what [spray](ReactorBuilding/Pressurizer/SprayOpen) can absorb by condensing steam, and it can also be opened manually from the control room console.

Discharge goes to the [pressurizer relief tank](ReactorBuilding/PressurizedReliefTank/Level), not directly to containment, where the steam condenses in a pool of water. The PORV earns its fame from failure mode rather than function: a valve commanded shut that sticks open turns the discharge path into a small LOCA, depressurizing the primary continuously - which is why position indication and downstream PRT response matter as much as the command itself.
