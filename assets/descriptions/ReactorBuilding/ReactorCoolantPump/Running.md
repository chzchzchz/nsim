Starts or stops the reactor coolant pumps (RCPs). During heatup the pumps are started sequentially and their heat input is deliberately used to raise RCS temperature toward hot standby.

At least one RCP is kept running to provide uniform cooldown of all loops until coolant temperature is below 160F (71C), after which natural circulation may take over. In hot standby a maximum of two out of four RCPs may be secured while proper loop flow is maintained with the rest.
