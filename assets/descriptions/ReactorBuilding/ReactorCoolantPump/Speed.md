Reactor coolant pump shaft speed in rpm. At full operation the pumps circulate roughly 100,000 gpm each through their loop; the large rotating mass also means flow decays slowly after a power loss.

Pump heat is a real heat source for the primary: primary temperature is maintained through balanced use of RCP heat input and steam dump. With pumps running and no fission heat, pump work alone can hold or slowly raise Tavg - useful at low power, dangerous if decay heat removal is misjudged.
