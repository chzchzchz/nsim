Steam-void fraction currently sitting in the steam generator feed ring, as a percent. Voids form only when SG levels sit below the tops of the feed rings and total feed flow falls below leakage out of the system, which the reference caps at 30 gallons per minute. The fraction accumulates while those conditions hold and decays when feed sweeps the ring.

It is the precondition for [HammerDamage](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/HammerDamage): a sudden high-flow step into a voided ring collapses the steam and hammers the feed nozzle sleeve. A creeping void with steady low feed is benign; the same void followed by an emergency feedwater start above 400 gallons per minute per generator is the hammer. Watch [Water](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/Water) and the feed flow together to see the void building.
