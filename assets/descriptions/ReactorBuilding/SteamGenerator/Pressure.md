Steam generator secondary steam pressure in psig, nominally about 1000 psig at full power falling toward roughly 800 psig at no-load as Tavg rises along the program.

Pressure is the handshake between primary and secondary: primary heat arriving versus steam being taken away sets it. Steam pressure held at saturation for no-load Tavg is precisely how hot standby is defined during startup.
