Primary-side heat currently transferred into this steam generator in watts. The SG is the dominant energy sink for the RCS; reducing this transfer - by isolating steam, losing level, or fouling tube surfaces - shows up quickly as rising Tavg.

At full power each generator absorbs about half the reactor's thermal output, corresponding to the rated 3,813,000 lb/hr steam flow per unit listed in the design data.
