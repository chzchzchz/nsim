Primary-side heat currently transferred into this steam generator in watts. The SG is the dominant energy sink for the RCS - essentially all core power plus reactor coolant pump heat flows through here on its way to the turbine or dump.

Reducing this transfer is the fastest way to stop a primary temperature drop once it starts (isolate steam, lose level), and losing it unexpectedly is the fastest way to a temperature rise: watch Tavg respond whenever HeatInput moves. At full power each generator absorbs about half of roughly 3,400 MW thermal, corresponding to the rated 3,813,000 lb/hr steam flow per unit in the design tables.
