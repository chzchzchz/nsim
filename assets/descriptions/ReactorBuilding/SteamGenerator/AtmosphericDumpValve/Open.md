Atmospheric dump valve position - the operator's manual handle for dumping SG steam straight to atmosphere. Use it when the turbine cannot take steam: turbine trip, load rejection, or black-grid conditions.

Power-operated relief and atmospheric dump valves are located on each steam line upstream of the stop valves, which is why they remain useful even after MSIV isolation. With the reactor tripped, matching atmospheric dump flow to decay heat is exactly how stable hot standby is held before RHR takes over.
