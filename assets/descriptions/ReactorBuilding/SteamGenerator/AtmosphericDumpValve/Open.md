Atmospheric dump valve position - the operator's manual handle for dumping SG steam straight to atmosphere when the normal sinks cannot take it: turbine trip, load rejection, loss of the condenser vacuum, or black-grid conditions with no place to send power at all.

Power-operated relief and atmospheric dump valves are located on each steam line upstream of the stop valves, which is why they remain useful even after MSIV isolation. With the reactor tripped, matching atmospheric dump flow to decay heat production is exactly how stable hot standby is held before RHR takes over cooling - the EFW system supplies feedwater to match whatever steam leaves this way.
