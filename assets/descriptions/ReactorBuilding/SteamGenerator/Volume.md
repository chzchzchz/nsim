Total fluid volume held by the steam generator - downcomer, tube bundle region, and steam space combined. Design data lists overall height around 67 ft 8 in for the reference four-loop units, making these some of the largest single components in the plant.

Volume matters during transients because of swell and shrink: when pressure drops, steam bubbles expand throughout the tube bundle region and displace water upward, moving apparent level far faster than any actual mass change. Operators reading [level](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/Water) during a transient are therefore reading volume behavior as much as inventory.
