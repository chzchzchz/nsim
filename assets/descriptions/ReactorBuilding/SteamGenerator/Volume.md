Total fluid volume held by the steam generator, including the downcomer, tube bundle, and steam space. Design data lists overall height around 67 ft 8 in for the reference four-loop units.

Volume matters during transients because swell and shrink - steam void changes in the tube bundle region - move apparent level far faster than actual mass changes when pressure shifts.
