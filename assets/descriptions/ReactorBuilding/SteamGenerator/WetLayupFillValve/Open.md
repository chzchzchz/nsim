Fills the steam generator secondary for wet layup - level established above the tube sheet so the primary-side tubes stay submerged in an oxygen-controlled chemistry environment while the unit is shut down.

Wet layup protects the tubes from air in exactly the geometry where they are thinnest. The fill is slow and chemistry-checked; once level is set, this valve's job is done until maintenance requires draining. It is a maintenance-only device - never part of a heat-removal lineup, and its position during power operation should simply be shut.

Model note: the ledger deliberately leaves this valve undrawn on the mimic; it is reachable as a bridge control for layup evolutions.
