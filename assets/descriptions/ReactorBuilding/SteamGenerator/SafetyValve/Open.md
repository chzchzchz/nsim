Self-actuating ASME spring-loaded safety valve in each SG outlet main steam line - secondary-side overpressure protection that needs neither power nor operator action. It lifts when steam pressure overcomes the spring, vents to atmosphere, and reseats when pressure falls back.

A lifted SG safety valve means secondary heat removal has fallen behind heat production for that generator. The diagnosis checklist reads straight down the steam supply chain: MSIV state, dump valve availability, turbine admission, feedwater level control. Unlike primary safeties discharging to a tank, these vent to atmosphere directly - inventory and activity consequences follow.
