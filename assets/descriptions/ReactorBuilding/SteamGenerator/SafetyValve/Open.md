Readback of the SG outlet main steam line safety valves' aggregate position as the model reports it; exposed mostly for visibility. The aggregate is the logical OR of the pressure law (pops at the model's assumed 985 psia setpoint, reseats about 3 percent below it) and the stuck-open latch — a bridge write to the fault injector ([SGSafetyValve](ReactorBuilding/Faults/SGSafetyValve)) holds it open until cleared.
