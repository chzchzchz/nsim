Self-actuating ASME spring-loaded safety valve in each SG outlet main steam line. It lifts on secondary overpressure without any signal or operator action and reseats when pressure falls back.

If these lift during an evolution, secondary heat removal has fallen behind heat production - check MSIV state, dump valves, turbine admission, and feedwater availability. The bridge exposes position mostly for visibility; simulation lifts the valve automatically at setpoint.
