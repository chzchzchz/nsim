Steam generator water inventory in gallons. The level control system maintains proper inventory by positioning the feedwater flow control valves; low-low level in two generators auto-starts emergency feedwater.

Inventory is the primary-to-secondary heat transfer medium - lose it and the primary loses its heat sink, which is why level deviations are among the most closely watched secondary parameters during transients.
