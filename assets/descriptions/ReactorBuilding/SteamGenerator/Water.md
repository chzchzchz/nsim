Steam generator water inventory in gallons. Level control maintains proper inventory by positioning the feedwater flow control valves against a program that follows load; the water is both the heat transfer medium to the primary and the reservoir that feeds steam production.

Losing it threatens everything downstream of the primary: without adequate level the primary loses its heat sink, Tavg climbs, and the reactor trips on high temperature or pressure if nothing else acts first. Low-low level in two of four channels in any two steam generators auto-starts [emergency feedwater](AuxiliaryBuilding/EmergencyFeedwaterSystem/SubsystemA/MotorPumpRunning), which is designed to restore minimum flow within a minute and hold it indefinitely.

Inventory readings also swing with transient effects - swell and shrink from pressure changes move apparent level faster than actual mass changes - so operators learn to read level trends against pressure simultaneously.
