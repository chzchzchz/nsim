Vents the steam line to atmosphere through a small-capacity path - the startup and cooldown valve for clearing air and non-condensables, and for bleeding pressure when the main paths are intentionally isolated.

During heatup, vents establish steam flow and strip air before any duty is put on the generator; during cooldown they give decay heat a place to go when turbine and condenser paths are lined out. The capacity is small on purpose: this is not a dump valve, and using it against significant decay heat is a slow, losing battle - [atmospheric dump](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/AtmosphericDumpValve/Open) is the sized path for that job.
