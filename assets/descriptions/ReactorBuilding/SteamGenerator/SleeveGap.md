Nozzle-sleeve gap in inches between the steam generator nozzle and the feed-ring sleeve. As-built it is 0.025 inches; erosion observed at Trojan grew it to 0.25 inches, which is the gap this damage state advances toward. Each hammer event moves the gap by its 5 percent share of the full range, and it caps at 0.25 inches after 20 events.

A wider gap is a wider leakage path for the void mechanism: backleakage through the gap plus system check-valve leakage is what holds the ring voided at low feed. Read it with [HammerDamage](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/HammerDamage) for the damage fraction and [FeedRingVoid](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/FeedRingVoid) for the current void it helps sustain. A gap already at 0.25 inches never widens further, so additional hammer events show up only in the damage fraction.
