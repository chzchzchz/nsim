Main steam isolation valve (MSIV) position on the SG outlet steam line. Closing it isolates that generator's secondary side from the turbine, steam dump, and everything downstream - the response to a steam line break inside or outside containment, or an operator-commanded isolation.

Isolation is a double-edged act: it stops the leak but also stops the heat removal path through that SG while fission and decay heat continue. Pressure in the isolated generator climbs until its [safety valves](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/SafetyValve/Open) lift or reactor power comes down; the other generators must pick up the slack or [atmospheric dump valves](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/AtmosphericDumpValve/Open) vent the excess.

Upstream of the stop valves sit the ASME safety valves and atmospheric dump valves, so even an isolated generator keeps overpressure protection and a controlled release path.
