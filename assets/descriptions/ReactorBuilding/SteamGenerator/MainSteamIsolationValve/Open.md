Main steam isolation valve (MSIV) position on the SG outlet steam line. Closing it isolates that generator's secondary side from the turbine, steam dump, and everything downstream - the response to a steam line break or an operator-commanded isolation.

Upstream of the stop valves sit the ASME safety valves and the atmospheric dump valves, so even with the MSIV shut the SG retains overpressure protection and a controlled release path. Isolating a generator while fission heat continues forces its pressure up until [safety valves](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/SafetyValve/Open) or reactor power reduction intervenes.
