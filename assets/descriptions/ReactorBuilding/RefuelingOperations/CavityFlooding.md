Fills the refueling cavity with borated water: "Reactor cavity is filled with borated water to the vessel flange." With the head off, this is what shields the core for open fuel handling and keeps it subcritical - the water is borated precisely so an assembly sitting in the cavity cannot approach criticality.

Set it after disassembly begins; setting it also clears [CavityDraining](ReactorBuilding/RefuelingOperations/CavityDraining), since the cavity cannot do both. Fuel moves only with the cavity up to flange level and [PoolTemp](AuxiliaryBuilding/SpentFuelPoolCoolingSystem/PoolTemp) under control - spent fuel stays underwater from the vessel to the storage pool.
