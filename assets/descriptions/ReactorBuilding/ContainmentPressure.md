Containment internal pressure in psig above atmosphere. Any release of steam or hot water inside containment - PORV discharge after disc burst, safety valve flow, or a break - shows up here first.

The passive containment design relies on condensing steam on internal structures to limit pressure; engineered systems such as containment spray actively knock it down when design targets are threatened. Rising pressure with known primary discharge is expected; rising pressure with no known release warrants immediate diagnosis.
