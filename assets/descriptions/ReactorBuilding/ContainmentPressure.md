Containment internal pressure in psig above atmosphere. Any release of steam or hot water inside containment - PORV discharge once the PRT rupture disc bursts, code safety flow, or a broken pipe - shows up here within seconds, because containment is a sealed building whose only outlet is engineered leakage paths.

The passive design leans on structure: steam condenses on the cool interior walls and structures, self-limiting pressure rise. Engineered responses back that up when design targets are threatened - [containment spray](AuxiliaryBuilding/ContainmentSpraySystem/SprayHeaderOpen) drenches the atmosphere with borated water that condenses steam and scrubs iodine. Rising pressure with a known discharge is expected bookkeeping; rising pressure with no known release source is an immediate diagnosis priority, since it implies a leak nobody has found yet.
