Reactor coolant loop isolation (block) valve position. These valves exist so a loop can be isolated from the RCS for maintenance or sectioning; they are fully open throughout normal power operation.

Closing a block valve with the plant at power would strand that loop's steam generator and pump, so it is an outage-only alignment verified during drain-down evolutions.
