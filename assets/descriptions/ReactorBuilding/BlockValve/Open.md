Reactor coolant loop isolation (block) valve position. These valves exist so an entire loop - its steam generator, pump, and piping - can be isolated from the RCS for maintenance; they are fully open throughout all normal power operation.

Closing one at power would strand that loop's SG and stop its pump's contribution, unbalancing Tavg computation and halving cooling margin, so it is strictly an outage-only alignment performed during drain-down evolutions under low-temperature low-pressure conditions. In the simulator the valve exists mainly for completeness of the RCS topology.
