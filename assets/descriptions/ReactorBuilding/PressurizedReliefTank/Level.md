Water level in the pressurizer relief tank in gallons. Rising level means the tank is doing its job - receiving condensed relief steam or PRT spray condensate - and its capacity is finite, sized to swallow the design relief inventory without overpressurizing.

Level climbing alongside rising tank pressure despite [spray](ReactorBuilding/PRTSprayValve/Open) is the prelude to [rupture disc](ReactorBuilding/PRTRuptureDiscBurst) failure and an unplanned path to containment. Post-transient, the tank needs refill, cooldown, and chemistry verification before it can protect another event; a PRT left half-empty is a latent hazard discovered only mid-transient.
