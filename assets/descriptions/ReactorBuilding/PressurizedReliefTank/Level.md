Water level in the pressurizer relief tank in gallons. Rising level means the tank is receiving condensed relief steam or PRT spray condensate; the tank is sized to condense the design relief inventory.

If level and pressure climb despite spray, expect the rupture disc to burst and route further discharge to containment. After any relief event the tank must be refilled and cooled before it can again accept a transient.
