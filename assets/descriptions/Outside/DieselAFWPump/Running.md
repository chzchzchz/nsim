Starts or stops the diesel-driven auxiliary feedwater pump—the most independent AFW option: the onsite emergency diesel generator supplies its own starting power, so this pump can operate even when station buses are dead after total loss of offsite power or safety injection actuation.

The diesel generator auto-starts within seconds of SI or LOOP; the operator may also command it manually when transitioning modes where AFW is already flowing but an extra pump is being positioned, or when confirming diesel availability before prolonging AFW-dependent operation. AFW flow to the steam generators is the decay-heat backstop once auxiliary cooling is lost.
