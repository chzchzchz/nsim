Service water return temperature after absorbing heat from the equipment coolers served. The rise above [inlet](Outside/ServiceWaterSystem/InletTemp) reflects total heat load being rejected; large contributors include [diesel jacket coolers](AuxiliaryBuilding/DieselGenerator/CoolantTemp) and CCW heat exchangers.

Runaway outlet temperature means heat load exceeds pumping capacity - respond by isolating nonessential loads ([NonessentialIsolated](Outside/ServiceWaterSystem/NonessentialIsolated)) or starting additional pumps before equipment starts tripping on its own high-temperature protection, in whatever order it happens to be wired.
