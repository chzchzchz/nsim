Service water return temperature after absorbing heat from the equipment coolers served. The rise above inlet reflects total heat load being rejected - diesel generator coolers are typical large contributors.

Runaway outlet temperature means heat load exceeds what the pumps and heat exchangers can move; isolate nonessential loads or add pumps before equipment trips on high temperatures.
