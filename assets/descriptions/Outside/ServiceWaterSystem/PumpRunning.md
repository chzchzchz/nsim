Essential service water pump running flag. SWS supplies all the plant's cooling water including emergency shutdown cooling - hence high reliability and redundancy requirements: three essential pumps per unit at roughly 20,000 gpm each.

Discharge passes through strainers into a common header feeding loop headers that give essential coolers dual supplies. With a pump running, model flow rises to design value and the diesel jacket coolers come alive - SWS health is prerequisite health for half the safety systems on site.
