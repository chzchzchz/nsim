Intake water temperature at the shared pump house, tracking river, lake, ocean, or basin conditions - shared intake with the circulating water pumps, so both systems see the same weather.

Warm intake water degrades two systems simultaneously: SWS has less margin to absorb equipment heat before hitting temperature limits, and the condenser loses vacuum as cold-side temperature rises. Summer peaks stress exactly the systems most needed during transients.
