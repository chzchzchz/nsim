Isolates the nonessential cooler branch headers from the main SWS headers to conserve water under accident conditions. Nonessential loads get trimmed so essential coolers - RHR exchangers, CCW heat exchangers, diesel coolers - keep full flow and pressure.

This is a deliberate operator action during events, enabled by branch headers designed separable from day one. Isolating early conserves inventory; isolating unnecessarily removes cooling from equipment that may still need it - judgment call under time pressure, which is why the lineup belongs in procedures.
