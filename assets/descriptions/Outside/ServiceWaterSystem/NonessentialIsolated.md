Isolates the nonessential cooler branch headers from the main SWS headers to conserve water under accident conditions. Nonessential loads get trimmed so essential coolers keep full flow and pressure.

This is an operator action during events - the valves exist precisely because the branch headers were deliberately separable from day one.
