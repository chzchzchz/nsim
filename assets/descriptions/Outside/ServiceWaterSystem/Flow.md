Service water header flow in gpm - the lumped readout of what running essential pumps are delivering to every cooler they serve: RHR exchangers, component cooling exchangers, diesel generator coolers, chillers.

Low flow with pumps running suggests strainer fouling (the reference strainers have 1/8-inch openings debris loves) or header problems. Downstream consequences stack quickly: [CCW outlet temperature](AuxiliaryBuilding/ComponentCoolingWaterSystem/OutletTemp) climbs first, then equipment trips on high temperatures arrive in cascade order.
