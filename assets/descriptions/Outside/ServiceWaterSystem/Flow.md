Service water header flow in gpm. The lumped readout of what the running essential pumps are delivering to every cooler they serve - RHR exchangers, CCW heat exchangers, diesel coolers.

Low flow with pumps running suggests strainer fouling or header problems; the reference strainers have 1/8-inch openings that debris loves to find.
