Circulating water pump running flag. The operator varies the number of CW pumps in service from the main control room, modulating discharge valves to match condenser heat load - more pumps in summer, fewer at night.

Depending on siting, the source may be river, lake, ocean, or cooling tower; intake bays with bar grills and traveling screens strip debris before the pumps see it. Losing CW pumps collapses condenser vacuum within minutes, forcing turbine trip and pushing the whole heat rejection burden onto steam dump and atmospheric relief.
