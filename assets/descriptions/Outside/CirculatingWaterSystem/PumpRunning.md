Circulating water pump running flag. The operator varies the number of CW pumps in service and modulates discharge valves from the main control room to match condenser heat load.

Depending on plant siting the source may be river, lake, ocean, or cooling tower; intake structure bays with bar grills and traveling screens strip debris before the pumps see the water.
