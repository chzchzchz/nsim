Circulating water flow through the main condensers in gpm - the final heat sink path for the entire plant. Everything the reactor produces, minus what the secondary cycle loses along the way, eventually leaves through this water into river, lake, sea, or sky.

The simplified model sizes two pumps at roughly 1.1 million gpm combined. Flow falling with pumps running suggests screen clogging, valve trouble, or suction loss - and since condenser pressure responds almost immediately, watch [vacuum](TurbineBuilding/Condenser/Pressure) deteriorate in step.
