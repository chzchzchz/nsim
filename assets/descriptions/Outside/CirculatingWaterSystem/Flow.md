Circulating water flow through the main condensers in gpm. This is the final heat sink path for the entire plant: everything the reactor makes eventually leaves through this water.

Losing CW pumps collapses condenser vacuum, forcing turbine trip and steam dump reliance within minutes; the simplified model sizes two pumps at roughly 1.1 million gpm combined.
