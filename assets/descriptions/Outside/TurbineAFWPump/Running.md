Starts or stops the turbine-driven auxiliary feedwater pump—the least electrical-dependent option: it runs by extracting steam from a main steam line, so it continues operating even after reactor trip with no station electrical supply.

The pump auto-starts on low-low SG water level to preserve steam generator inventory against decay heat; the operator may also start it manually when pre-positioning AFW before conditions warrant auto-start, or when taking another pump offline. [SG pressure](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/Pressure) must be sufficient for turbine admission.
