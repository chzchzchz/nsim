Starts or stops the motor-driven auxiliary feedwater pump, fed from Safety Injection system ESF bus A. After loss of offsite power, the emergency diesel generators take over the plant distribution bus, so ESF bus A retains power as long as one diesel is running.

The operator may start this pump before safety injection actuation when maneuvering into hot standby with AFW already supplying, or may take it offline once the turbine-driven pump is confirmed running. The steam generators rely on minimum required flow within one minute of demand, so redundant pump availability matters before you surrender it.
