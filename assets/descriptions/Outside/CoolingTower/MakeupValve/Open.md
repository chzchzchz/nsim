Cooling tower makeup valve position, admitting replacement water for inventory lost to evaporation and drift plus continuous blowdown. Two makeup pumps serve this duty in the reference design.

Under-makeup slowly drains the basin until CW pumps start cavitating; over-makeup wastes water and chemicals. Balance against measured [evaporation](Outside/CoolingTower/EvaporationFlow) and [blowdown](Outside/CoolingTower/BlowdownValve/Open).
