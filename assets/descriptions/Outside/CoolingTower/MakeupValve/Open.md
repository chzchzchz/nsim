Cooling tower makeup valve position, admitting replacement water for inventory lost to evaporation, drift, and deliberate blowdown. Two makeup pumps serve this duty in the reference design; makeup runs nearly continuously because evaporation never stops.

Under-makeup slowly drains the [basin](Outside/CoolingTower/Basin/Level) until CW pumps start cavitating; over-makeup wastes water and chemicals. Balance against measured [evaporation](Outside/CoolingTower/EvaporationFlow) plus [blowdown](Outside/CoolingTower/BlowdownValve/Open) - the arithmetic should close every shift.
