Water evaporated into the atmosphere by the tower in gpm, with drift loss folded in. Evaporation is where the condenser heat actually goes - latent heat of vaporization carries away essentially all the energy the plant produced, one gallon at a time.

Every gallon evaporated must be replaced by [makeup](Outside/CoolingTower/MakeupValve/Open), while the dissolved solids left behind are managed by [blowdown](Outside/CoolingTower/BlowdownValve/Open). Evaporation rate rises with load and with hot dry weather, which is why makeup demand is seasonal.
