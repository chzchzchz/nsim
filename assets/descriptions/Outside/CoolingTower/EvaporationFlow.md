Water evaporated into the atmosphere by the tower in gpm, with drift loss folded in. Evaporation is where the condenser heat actually goes - latent heat of vaporization carries it away.

Every gallon evaporated must be replaced by [makeup](Outside/CoolingTower/MakeupValve/Open), while dissolved solids left behind are managed by [blowdown](Outside/CoolingTower/BlowdownValve/Open).

Related controls: [MakeupValve](Outside/CoolingTower/MakeupValve/Open), [BlowdownValve](Outside/CoolingTower/BlowdownValve/Open)
Related sensors: [ColdBasinTemp](Outside/CoolingTower/ColdBasinTemp)
