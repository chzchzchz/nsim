Cooling tower basin inventory in gallons. Cooled water falls into the basin - about 5.2 million gallons of capacity in the reference design - which serves directly as CW pump suction.

Below minimum suction level the CW pumps lose suction and condenser heat rejection collapses, which makes this quiet outdoor number one of the most consequential levels in the plant. [Makeup control](Outside/CoolingTower/MakeupValve/Open) exists precisely to defend it against the continuous theft of [evaporation](Outside/CoolingTower/EvaporationFlow).
