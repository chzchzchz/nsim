Temperature of cooled water collected in the tower basin and returned to the condenser. This sets the cold end of the condenser heat transfer and therefore achievable vacuum.

Ambient wet-bulb temperature bounds performance - a tower cannot cool below it, so hot humid days quietly cost output even with everything mechanically perfect.
