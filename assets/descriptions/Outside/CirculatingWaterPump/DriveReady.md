Indicates upstream bus voltage is available so the circulating water pump motor can start - the same upstream-power gating applied to safety drives, applied here to the biggest motors in the plant.

CW pump motors ride on the plant distribution fed by the unit or system auxiliary transformer, so switching events elsewhere can silently remove CW capability while everything looks fine locally.
