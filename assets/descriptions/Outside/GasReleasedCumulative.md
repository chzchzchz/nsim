One-way cumulative activity discharged to the atmosphere through the plant vent, in becquerels. It never decreases, so the trend is the release record over the shift or the outage. Subtracting earlier readings gives the release over any interval.

Use it to judge total exposure from a transient rather than chasing the instantaneous [PlantVentActivity](Outside/PlantVentActivity) spike. During normal operation it should accumulate slowly from the charcoal-delay decay tail; fast accumulation means [GasHoldup](Outside/GasHoldup) inventory is flushing out, usually after a primary leak or a containment pathway opening. Pair it with the liquid cumulative to rank which pathway dominated the event. A release that barely moves this counter while the vent spikes are brief charcoal-surge pass-throughs, not sustained transfers.
