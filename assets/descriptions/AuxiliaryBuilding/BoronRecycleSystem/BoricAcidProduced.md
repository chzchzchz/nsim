Cumulative boric acid concentrate produced, in gallons, at the evaporator's product grade of 4 weight percent (40,000 ppm by mass). This is one of the two payoffs of reclamation: boron recovered for reuse in the RCS.

The concentrates are normally pumped to the boric acid tanks through the recycle evaporator concentrates filter; if they cannot be discharged there, the reference allows diverting them back to the recycle holdup tanks. Production accumulates while [FeedPumpRunning](AuxiliaryBuilding/BoronRecycleSystem/FeedPumpRunning) is on and feed ([FeedFlow](AuxiliaryBuilding/BoronRecycleSystem/FeedFlow)) processes, independent of the distillate side unless a [Carryover](AuxiliaryBuilding/Faults/EvaporatorCarryover) divert is also running.
