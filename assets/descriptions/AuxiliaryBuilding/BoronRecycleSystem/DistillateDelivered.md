Cumulative clean distillate delivered to the reactor makeup water storage tank, in gallons. This is the other payoff of reclamation: primary-grade water recovered from letdown and available again for makeup.

Delivery runs through the evaporator condensate demineralizer and condensate filter, with the scintillation counter guarding the last stretch of line into the tank. A high radiation signal stops this counter: [DistillateDiverted](AuxiliaryBuilding/BoronRecycleSystem/DistillateDiverted) latches and accumulated product waits as [Recirculating](AuxiliaryBuilding/BoronRecycleSystem/Recirculating) inventory until the monitor clears. Comparing it against [BoricAcidProduced](AuxiliaryBuilding/BoronRecycleSystem/BoricAcidProduced) shows how a batch of processed effluent split between water and boron.
