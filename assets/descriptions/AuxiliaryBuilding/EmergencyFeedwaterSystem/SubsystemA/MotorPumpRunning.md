Emergency feedwater motor-driven pump for subsystem A. When normal feedwater is lost these pumps keep the steam generators fed so decay heat removal continues; all discharge valves stand open permanently, so a start delivers water immediately with no lineup delay - that is why the analysis can credit minimum required flow within one minute of actuation to at least two effective steam generators, continuing indefinitely without operator action.

The first 30 minutes assume no operator help at all; only afterward do credit-taking procedures have anyone touching flow modulating valves to trim SG levels back into the narrow control band. With the reactor tripped and EFW matching steam removal of decay heat plus one RCP's heat input, the plant sits in stable hot standby indefinitely - this balance is the design anchor of the entire system.
