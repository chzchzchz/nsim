Emergency feedwater motor-driven pump for subsystem A. When normal feedwater is lost these pumps feed the steam generators so decay heat removal continues; all discharge valves stand open so a start delivers flow immediately.

The system is designed to supply at least minimum required flow within one minute of actuation to at least two effective steam generators and continue indefinitely without operator action. For the first assumed 30 minutes no operator credit is taken; afterward flow modulating valves trim level into the narrow control band.
