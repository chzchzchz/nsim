Steam-driven emergency feedwater pump for subsystem A. It auto-starts on low-low level in two of four level channels in any two steam generators, driven by extracted steam - which is the point: it works on station blackout, when every motor-driven pump on site is silent.

Turbine-driven pumps are the defense-in-depth half of EFWS: independent driver, independent steam supply, same immediate-delivery philosophy. Their exhaust goes up a dedicated stack; you can hear them start from the parking lot, which operators regard as reassuring.
