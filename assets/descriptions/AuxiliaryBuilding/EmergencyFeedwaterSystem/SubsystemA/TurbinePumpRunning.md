Steam-driven emergency feedwater pump for subsystem A. It auto-starts on low-low level in two of four SG level channels in any two steam generators, driven by extracted steam so it works even with total loss of AC power.

Turbine-driven pumps are the defense-in-depth half of EFWS: independent driver, independent steam supply, same immediate-delivery philosophy as the motor pumps.
