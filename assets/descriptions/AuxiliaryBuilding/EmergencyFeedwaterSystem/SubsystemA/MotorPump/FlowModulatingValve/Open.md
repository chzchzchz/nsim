Manual flow control on the subsystem A motor-driven emergency feedwater pump discharge. After an AFW actuation the valve is assumed wide open for the first 30 minutes of injection; from then on the operator positions it to match decay-heat steam demand on the receiving steam generator.

AFW flow control is deliberately a manual station: "the pumps are manually started and the flowrates are manually controlled by positioning the flow modulating valves." Too little flow starves a hot generator; too much overfills it and carries water out the main steam path. Watch the target [SG level](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/Water) while throttling, and remember the recirculation line keeps the pump protected at shut-in.
