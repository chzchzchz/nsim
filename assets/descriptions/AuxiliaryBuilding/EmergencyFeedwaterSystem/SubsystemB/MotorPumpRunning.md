Emergency feedwater motor-driven pump for subsystem B - the redundant twin of subsystem A's motor pump, powered from the opposite ESF train so no single electrical failure disables both.

The subsystem pairing exists because the accident analysis needs at least two steam generators fed regardless of any one failure: one pump, one bus, one valve line-up. Like its A-train sibling it delivers through permanently open discharge paths the moment it starts.
