Steam-driven emergency feedwater pump for subsystem B, auto-starting on low-low SG level exactly like its A-train sibling but drawing on the B train's power and steam sources - so the pair survives any single failure anywhere in protection, power, or steam supply.

Between motor and turbine pumps across both subsystems, at least two effective generators stay fed within a minute of demand. The steam supply is deliberately taken upstream of the main turbine: even a tripped, isolated turbine leaves plenty of decay-heat steam to drive a feedwater pump.
