Diesel fuel oil storage remaining in gallons. Each unit carries a day tank plus storage sized for continuous rated load operation for a minimum of seven days without resupply.

Fuel systems have instrumentation to annunciate abnormal conditions alongside air start and generator output monitoring - fuel exhaustion during an accident is a slow-motion loss of the entire backup power train.
