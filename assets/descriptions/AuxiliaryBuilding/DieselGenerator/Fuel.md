Diesel fuel oil storage remaining in gallons. Each unit carries a day tank plus bulk storage sized for continuous rated load operation for a minimum of seven days without resupply - accidents are assumed long, and resupply assumed difficult.

Fuel systems have instrumentation to annunciate abnormal conditions alongside the air-start and generator monitoring systems, so level trends should never surprise. Slow leaks discovered by trend instead of alarm are the difference between routine procurement and an emergency contractor.
