Fractional running state of the diesel engine from stopped (0) to at rated speed and voltage (1) - the cranking-to-ready progress bar.

The 30-second design requirement covers this whole ramp; a diesel that cannot reach rated conditions in time fails its safety function regardless of how healthy it looks otherwise.
