Fractional running state of the diesel engine from stopped (0) to at rated conditions (1) - effectively the cranking-to-ready progress bar compressed into one number.

The 30-second design window covers this whole ramp: cranking, acceleration, governor stabilization, excitation buildup. A diesel that reaches rated conditions in 45 seconds fails its safety function even though it runs fine afterward, so watching how quickly this fraction climbs during periodic tests is the real acceptance test.
