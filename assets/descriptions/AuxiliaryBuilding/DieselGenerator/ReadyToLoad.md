Indicates the diesel is at rated voltage and frequency with breaker available, ready to accept load - the go/no-go light for transferring ESF loads onto the machine.

Capability to carry rated load within 30 seconds of the start signal is the acceptance criterion; ReadyToLoad appearing late is de facto failure even if the engine eventually runs. When it lights during a real event, load sequencing follows the accident analysis order - pumps first, valves as needed - not convenience.
