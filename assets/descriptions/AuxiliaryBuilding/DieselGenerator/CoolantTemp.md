Diesel jacket water coolant temperature in Fahrenheit. The engine's own heat is rejected through a jacket water cooler served by [service water](Outside/ServiceWaterSystem/Flow), so SWS trouble shows up here within minutes under load.

Standby diesels are kept warm deliberately: a cold block thermal-shocks under instant rated load, and the 30-second start requirement leaves no warmup time. Coolant temperature drifting down at standby means heat tracing or pre-lube trouble; climbing under load means jacket cooling is losing its [service water](Outside/ServiceWaterSystem/PumpRunning) supply.
