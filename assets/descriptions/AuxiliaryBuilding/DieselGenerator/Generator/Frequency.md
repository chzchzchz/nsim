Diesel generator output frequency; nominal 60 Hz feeding its ESF bus. Frequency tracks engine speed, so this reading doubles as confirmation the governor has settled after the start transient.

Loads transferred onto the bus see frequency directly - motor-driven pumps run slow on low frequency, moving less cooling water than their data sheets promise. Watch it stabilize before crediting the train.
