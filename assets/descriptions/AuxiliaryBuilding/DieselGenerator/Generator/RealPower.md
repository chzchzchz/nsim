Real power the diesel generator is delivering to its ESF bus. Each machine is sized for the engineered safety features loads assigned to the bus it feeds; loading beyond design margin on a machine already at rated output risks the whole train mid-accident.

Instrumentation monitors all important generator parameters and annunciates abnormal conditions - real power versus expected load sequence tells you whether automatic load shedding and sequencing worked as designed.
