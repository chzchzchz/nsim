Closes the diesel generator output breaker onto its engineered safety features bus. This is the moment backup power becomes actual power for the safety loads - pumps do not care that a diesel spun up if the breaker stayed open.

Closing requires the generator matched to bus voltage and frequency (its own regulator handles that within seconds of RatedToLoad... ReadyToLoad), and the bus dead or properly separated from off-site sources so the two supplies never parallel unintentionally. Each source feeds its bus through physically and electrically independent paths up to this connection point.
