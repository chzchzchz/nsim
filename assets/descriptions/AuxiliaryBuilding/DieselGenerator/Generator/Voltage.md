Diesel generator output voltage. Static brushless excitation with field flashing gives rapid voltage buildup after a start - field flashing forces initial current into the exciter so voltage rises in seconds rather than stalling near zero.

Voltage matters downstream because every ESF motor sized for 6600 V or 480 V needs it within tolerance to develop torque; a diesel at correct frequency but depressed voltage produces pumps that hum instead of pump.
