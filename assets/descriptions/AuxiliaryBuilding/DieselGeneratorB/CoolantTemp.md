Train B diesel jacket water coolant temperature in Fahrenheit. Like its A-side twin, the engine rejects its own heat through a jacket water cooler served by [service water](Outside/ServiceWaterSystem/Flow), so SWS trouble on this train shows up here within minutes under load.

The standby machine is kept warm deliberately - a cold block thermal-shocks under instant rated load, and the 30-second start requirement leaves no warmup time. Temperature drifting down at standby means heat tracing or pre-lube trouble on this train; climbing under load means jacket cooling is losing service water.
