Fractional running state of the train B diesel from stopped (0) to at rated conditions (1) - the cranking-to-ready progress bar in one number.

The 30-second design window covers the whole ramp: cranking, acceleration, governor stabilization, excitation buildup. Watching how quickly this fraction climbs during periodic surveillance is the real acceptance test for this machine; a slow climb that still ends at 1.0 has already failed its safety function mid-event.
