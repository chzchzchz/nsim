Train B diesel at rated voltage and frequency with breaker available - the go/no-go light for transferring this train's ESF loads onto the machine.

Carrying rated load within 30 seconds of the start signal is the acceptance criterion; [ReadyToLoad](AuxiliaryBuilding/DieselGeneratorB/ReadyToLoad) appearing late is de facto failure even if the engine eventually runs. When it lights during a real event, load sequencing on this train follows the accident analysis order - pumps first, valves as needed.
