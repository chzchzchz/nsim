Starts the residual heat removal train TrainB pump. The RHRS takes over shutdown cooling once RCS conditions permit - temperature dropped to 350F and pressure below roughly 400 psig, because RHR piping is low-pressure design and cannot see full RCS pressure - and brings the plant from there to cold shutdown, reaching 140F within about 20 hours with both trains running.

Each pump takes suction from an RCS hot leg through its own line and discharges through the RHR heat exchanger against component cooling water, returning cooled flow to the cold legs via shared SI piping. Expect a miniflow warmup period first: running hot reactor coolant into cold pipework thermally shocks it, so the pump spins on its bypass circuit until temperatures equalize.
