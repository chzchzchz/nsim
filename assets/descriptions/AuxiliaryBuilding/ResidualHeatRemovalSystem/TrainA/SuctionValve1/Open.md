RHR train TrainA suction valve 1 position. Opening aligns an RCS loop to the RHR pump suction; the block valves are opened only after the pump has warmed on miniflow and CCW is established to the exchanger.

These valves are part of the RCS pressure boundary during RHR operation, so their status defines whether the train can actually take suction - pump running with suction shut is a dead-headed pump.
