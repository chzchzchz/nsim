RHR train TrainA suction valve 1 position. Opening aligns an RCS loop to the RHR pump suction; the block valves are opened only after the pump has warmed on miniflow and CCW is established to the exchanger.

These valves sit in the RCS pressure boundary during RHR operation, so their status defines whether the train can actually take suction - a running pump with suction shut is a dead-headed pump with no place to send its flow except its relief valve.
