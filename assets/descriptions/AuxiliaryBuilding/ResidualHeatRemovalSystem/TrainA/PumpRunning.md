Starts the residual heat removal train TrainA pump. The RHRS takes over shutdown cooling once RCS conditions permit - temperature dropped to 350F and pressure below roughly 400 psig - and brings the plant from there to cold shutdown, reaching 140F within about 20 hours with both trains running.

Each pump takes suction from an RCS hot leg through its own line, pumps through the RHR heat exchanger against component cooling water, and returns to the cold legs via shared SI piping. Expect a miniflow warmup period first so hot coolant does not thermally shock pump and exchanger.
