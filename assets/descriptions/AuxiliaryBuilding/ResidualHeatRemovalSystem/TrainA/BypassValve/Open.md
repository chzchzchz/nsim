RHR train TrainA bypass valve position. Modulating flow around the RHR heat exchanger controls how much heat each pass removes - this is the cooldown-rate handle, since primary heatup rate is limited to about 50F/hr by spec and cooldown rates are similarly bounded.

Full bypass sends minimal flow through the exchanger (slow cooldown); closing bypass maximizes heat transfer. Watch component cooling water supply temperature, which caps exchanger performance.
