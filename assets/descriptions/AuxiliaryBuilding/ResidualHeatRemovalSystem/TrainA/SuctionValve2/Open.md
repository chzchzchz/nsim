RHR train TrainA suction valve 2 position, the second of two valves in that train's suction path from the RCS hot leg.

Both must be open for cooling flow. They are operated only under low-temperature low-pressure conditions per the RHR lineup sequence - opening them against a pressurized primary would put low-pressure design piping across full system pressure through an open path.
