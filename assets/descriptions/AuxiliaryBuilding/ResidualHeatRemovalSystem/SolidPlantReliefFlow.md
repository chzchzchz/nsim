Flow the solid-plant pressure valve is passing, in gpm - the live answer to "how far off is the primary from its selected pressure right now" during water-solid cooldown.

Steady small flow means the valve is doing its job against normal decay-heat shrink and swell. Flow climbing without a temperature change means something is adding energy or volume (a mispositioned heater or charging throttle); flow pinned wide with pressure still rising means the valve is out of authority and the next stop is the RHR overpressure review before continuing the cooldown.

Model note: derived from the valve's overshoot against the selected setpoint; the spec describes the function but states no flow figure.
