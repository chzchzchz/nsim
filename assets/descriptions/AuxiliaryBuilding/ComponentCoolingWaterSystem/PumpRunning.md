Component cooling water pump running indicator. CCW is the intermediate loop: it cools radioactive or primary-side equipment (RHR exchangers, diesel coolers, pump seals) so service water never touches potentially contaminated systems.

The design provides two separate redundant safeguards trains with no cross-connection, plus an auxiliary subsystem giving 100 percent cooling for normal operation - pump state tells you which arrangement you are in.
