Component cooling water pump running indicator. CCW is the intermediate loop between potentially radioactive equipment and the great outdoors: it cools RHR heat exchangers, diesel coolers, pump seals, and spent fuel pool exchangers so service water never touches primary-side systems directly.

The design provides two physically separate redundant safeguards trains plus an auxiliary subsystem delivering 100 percent cooling for normal operation - which arrangement you are in decides what a pump trip means. Losing CCW cascades fast: RHR exchangers stop transferring, shutdown cooling stalls, seal cooling vanishes.
