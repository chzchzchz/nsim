Component cooling water temperature returning from the cooled equipment in Fahrenheit. It aggregates every heat load CCW serves; rising outlet temperature means total load exceeds heat removal capacity.

Since RHR heat exchanger performance depends on CCW supply temperature, this reading directly caps achievable cooldown rate during shutdown cooling.
