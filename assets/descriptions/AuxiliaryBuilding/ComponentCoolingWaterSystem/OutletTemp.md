Component cooling water temperature returning from the cooled equipment in Fahrenheit. It aggregates every heat load CCW serves, so rising outlet temperature means total demand exceeds removal capacity - find the new load or add cooling.

Since RHR heat exchanger performance depends on CCW supply temperature, this reading caps achievable cooldown rate during shutdown cooling: hot CCW means slow cooldown no matter how the [bypass valves](AuxiliaryBuilding/ResidualHeatRemovalSystem/TrainA/BypassValve/Open) are set.
