Programmed pressurizer water level as percent of range - the moving target the level controller chases. The program rises with Tavg because water expands roughly 2 percent between cold and hot conditions; a plant that kept level fixed would find its pressurizer alternately overflowing and emptying as power changed.

Reading programmed next to actual gives the instantaneous level error. Divergence that persists after transients settle points at real inventory trouble - a leak feeding the containment, or a CVCS that cannot keep up.
