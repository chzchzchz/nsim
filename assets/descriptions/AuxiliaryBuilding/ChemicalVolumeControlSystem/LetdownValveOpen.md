Opens the letdown line, letting reactor coolant flow out of the RCS toward the CVCS for cleanup, chemistry adjustment, and volume management. Letdown is half of the inventory seesaw whose other half is charging.

The reference startup uses it explicitly: operators raise letdown flow to pull [pressurizer level](ReactorBuilding/Pressurizer/Water) down into the 20-80 percent band while the steam bubble forms, since level must fall before heatup creates expansion room. Letdown also passes through regenerative and letdown heat exchangers - recovering heat into charging water - so big step changes perturb downstream temperatures too.
