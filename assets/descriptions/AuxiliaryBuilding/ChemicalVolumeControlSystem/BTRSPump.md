Runs the boron thermal regeneration system pump, circulating letdown flow through the BTRS beds where boric acid is reversibly absorbed or released depending on bed temperature.

BTRS is the elegant middle path between dedicated boration and dilution: the same equipment can either remove boron from letdown (concentrating) or give it back (diluting) without changing tank inventories, executed through temperature swings of the beds.
