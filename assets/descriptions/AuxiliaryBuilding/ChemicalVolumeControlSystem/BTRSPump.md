Runs the boron thermal regeneration system pump, circulating letdown flow through the BTRS beds where boric acid is reversibly absorbed or released depending on bed temperature.

BTRS is the elegant middle path between dedicated boration and dilution tanks: the same column of resin can either strip boron out of flowing coolant (cool bed, absorbing) or give it back (warm bed, releasing). Temperature swings do the work, so small concentration corrections never have to touch tank inventories or discharge anything - the boron just waits inside the plant for reuse.
