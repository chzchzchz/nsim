Saturation of the boron thermal regeneration demineralizer bed as a percent. A saturated bed can no longer absorb boron, silently degrading boration capability until chemistry support is lost.

Temperature-swing regeneration is the designed response; if saturation climbs while BTRS should be absorbing, verify bed temperatures and flow alignment.
