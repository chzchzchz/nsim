Saturation of the boron thermal regeneration demineralizer bed as a percent. The bed works by reversible absorption: cool it and it soaks up boric acid from letdown, warm it and the boron releases back into flowing water. Saturation climbing while the bed should be absorbing means boration capability is quietly evaporating.

Temperature-swing regeneration is the designed response - no chemicals consumed, nothing discharged. If saturation rises despite correct temperatures and flows, suspect bed exhaustion or channeling; either way, boration margin is now a number to watch rather than assume.
