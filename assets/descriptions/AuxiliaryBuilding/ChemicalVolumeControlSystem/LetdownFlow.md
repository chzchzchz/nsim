Letdown line flow out of the RCS in gpm - the drain side of CVCS circulation. Raising letdown is the standard trick for lowering pressurizer level ahead of heatup: with charging held constant, extra outflow walks level down to the 25 percent starting point the reference procedure targets.

Everything let down passes through regenerative and letdown heat exchangers, then purification demineralizers, so letdown doubles as the plant's blood filtration loop - chemistry samples come from here. Step changes also move heat around the CVCS, so gentle beats abrupt.
