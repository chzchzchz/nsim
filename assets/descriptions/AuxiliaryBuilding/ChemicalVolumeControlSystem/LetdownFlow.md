Letdown line flow out of the RCS in gpm. Raising letdown is the standard trick for lowering pressurizer level ahead of heatup; reducing it hands inventory growth back to charging control.

Letdown passes through regeneration and letdown heat exchangers en route to the volume control tank, so big step changes also perturb CVCS temperatures.
