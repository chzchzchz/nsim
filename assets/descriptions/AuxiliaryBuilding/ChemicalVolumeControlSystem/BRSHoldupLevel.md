Holdup tank level in the boron recycle system, gallons. Recycled letdown, equipment drains, and aerated wastes collect here before processing decides their fate: reuse as makeup, or route to waste processing.

Inventory accumulating faster than expected usually means letdown exceeds what the cycle can reabsorb - check [letdown flow](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/LetdownFlow) against charging. A full holdup tank forces diversion to waste processing, which costs processed-water inventory somewhere else.
