Holdup tank level in the boron recycle system, gallons. Recycled letdown and equipment drains collect here before processing or reuse as makeup.

Inventory accumulating faster than expected usually means letdown exceeds what the cycle can reabsorb - check [letdown flow](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/LetdownFlow) against charging.
