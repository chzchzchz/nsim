Charging line flow into the RCS in gpm. Together with letdown it defines net primary inventory movement, and its floor is set by RCP seal injection requirements - charging must never starve the seals.

Watch it respond to pressurizer level error under automatic control: persistent deviation means the level program moved (heatup) or a leak exists.
