Automatic pressurizer level control: charging flow adjusts continuously to hold level at the programmed value, which itself varies with RCS average coolant temperature - hotter water expands, so the program asks for more steam-space inventory as power rises.

Under manual control the operator does this matching by hand. Workable steady-state; tedious through heatup when the target is itself moving. The reference startup sequence specifically calls out switching charging pump control to automatic once no-load level is reached - one of several handoffs that turn a startup into something two people can run.
