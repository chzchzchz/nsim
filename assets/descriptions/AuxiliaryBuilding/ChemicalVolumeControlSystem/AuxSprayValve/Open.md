Position of the auxiliary spray valve, the CVCS bypass around the normal pressurizer spray. It routes throttled charging flow into the pressurizer steam space for pressure control when the reactor coolant pumps are secured and normal spray is unavailable.

Opening it is a cooldown act: spray condenses steam in the pressurizer and drives pressure and level down together, so watch spray rate against the 100 F/h cooldown limit. The valve does nothing unless the [charging pump](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/ChargingPumpRunning) is running - effective delivery is reported separately as [AuxSprayDrive](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/AuxSprayDrive).
