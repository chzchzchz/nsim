Manual throttle on charging flow while the pump runs outside automatic level control. 0 means unthrottled design flow (30 gpm); intermediate values scale the delivery into the cold legs.

This is the fine adjustment for matching letdown during maneuvers: charging and letdown flow control hold primary inventory and pressure together, and the operator trims demand here rather than cycling the pump. "The charging pump flow and low-pressure letdown valve are controlled manually during plant startup and cooldown" - exactly the phases where this control lives.
