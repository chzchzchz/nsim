Runs a CVCS charging pump injecting water from the volume control tank into the cold legs. During startup, charging pump speed is reduced to the minimum allowable RCP seal injection flow, then switched to automatic once pressurizer level reaches no-load setpoint.

Charging versus [letdown](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/LetdownFlow) is the inventory seesaw: the pressurizer level control system adjusts charging automatically to track programmed level as Tavg moves.
