Engages automatic boron trim: when on, rod-bank travel position selects the BTRS mode so chemistry keeps up with reactivity changes the control rods can no longer absorb alone.

As fuel burns and boron dilutes over a cycle, the rods run out of withdrawal margin during load follow. Automatic boron control watches the rod insertion pattern and blends boration or dilution through the [charging path](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/ChargingPumpRunning) to restore margin without operator arithmetic. Leave it off for manual chemistry exercises; leave it on and watch [BoronTarget](AuxiliaryBuilding/ChemicalVolumeControlSystem/BoronTarget) instead of chasing setpoints by hand.
