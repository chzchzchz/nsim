Indicates upstream power is available from the ESF-fed 480 V MCC so the spent fuel pool cooling pumps can start. No bus voltage, no cooling, regardless of pump health.

This is the same upstream-gating philosophy applied to other safety loads: check power availability before crediting any pump indication.
