Spent fuel pool cooling pump running indicator. Normal operation requires two pumps running with the third in standby; decay heat from irradiated fuel keeps arriving whether anyone watches or not, and the pool is the only thing standing between it and boiling.

Pools are long-memory systems: assemblies removed weeks ago still carry meaningful heat. Losing SFPCS starts a slow clock toward boiling and evaporation - slower than most transients, but just as real, and complicated by radiation levels that make late intervention unpleasant.
