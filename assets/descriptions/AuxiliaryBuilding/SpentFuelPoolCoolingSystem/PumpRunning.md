Spent fuel pool cooling pump running indicator. Normal operation requires two pumps running with the third in standby; decay heat from irradiated fuel must be removed continuously or pool temperature climbs toward boiling.

The pool is a long-memory system: even weeks after shutdown, fuel assemblies carry enough decay heat that losing SFPCS cooling starts a clock toward boiling and evaporation.
