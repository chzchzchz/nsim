Pool-water turbidity as a percent normalized to the design crud burst: 0 percent is clear water, 100 percent is the suspended-solids load the cleanup side was sized against.

Clarity gates refueling speed - heavy water robs operators of visual access to the fuel during handling, which the spec treats as part of the system's job of "maintain[ing] the clarity and purity" of pool and refueling water. A jump to 100 percent means a crud burst just happened (see [CrudBurst](AuxiliaryBuilding/SpentFuelPoolCoolingSystem/CrudBurst)); recovery is purification ([RWPUPurifying](AuxiliaryBuilding/SpentFuelPoolCoolingSystem/RWPUPurifying)) run until the number decays back down.
