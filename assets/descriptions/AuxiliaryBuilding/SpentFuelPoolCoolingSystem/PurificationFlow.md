Cleanup flow through the filter and demineralizer loop, about 100 gpm per loop. Cooling and purification ride together: part of each pump's flow branches through cleanup en route back to the pool, holding chemistry and clarity.

Falling purification flow with steady cooling points at strainer clogging or diverted valves. Pool chemistry depends on this side stream - clarity matters operationally too, since refueling crews need to see what they are doing under eleven meters of water.
