Cleanup flow through the filter and demineralizer loop, about 100 gpm per loop. Cooling and purification run together: the same pumps push part of their flow through cleanup en route back to the pool.

Falling purification flow with steady cooling points at strainer clogging - chemistry control of the pool depends on this side stream.
