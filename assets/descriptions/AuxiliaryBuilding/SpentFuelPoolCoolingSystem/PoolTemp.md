Spent fuel pool water temperature, F. The pool exists to strip decay heat from irradiated fuel: the cooling system is "designed to remove residual decay heat generated by irradiated fuel stored in the spent fuel pool."

The trend is the message. A steady reading says the heat exchangers match the decaying load; a slow climb with pumps running points at cooling trouble (loss of flow, fouled [HeaderPressure](AuxiliaryBuilding/SpentFuelPoolCoolingSystem/HeaderPressure) support), and an uncooled pool walks toward boiling, driving off inventory. On the plant mimic the pool washes toward orange as it warms.

Model note: the 120 to 200 F color band is simulator-derived; the reference describes the duty without quoting an operating temperature band.
