Alignment switch for the refueling water purification train: routes pool and refueling-canal water through the purification pump and filter, which the spec notes "can be used separately or in conjunction with the spent fuel pool demineralizer."

Use it after anything that fouls the water - a [CrudBurst](AuxiliaryBuilding/SpentFuelPoolCoolingSystem/CrudBurst), debris from fuel movement - and watch [Turbidity](AuxiliaryBuilding/SpentFuelPoolCoolingSystem/Turbidity) fall as the filter cleans up. Clarity matters operationally: refueling proceeds at the speed operators can see through the water.
