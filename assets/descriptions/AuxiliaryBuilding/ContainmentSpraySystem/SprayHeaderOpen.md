Containment spray header valves open. Actuated spray delivers borated sodium hydroxide solution into upper containment, condensing steam directly and entraining airborne iodine as droplets carry it to the floor - pressure control and fission product cleanup in one system.

Storage tanks provide roughly 30 minutes of spray with additive; when the tank drains to its Lo-Lo setpoint, operators manually switch pumps to recirculation sump suction for long-term operation. Open headers with running pumps should visibly bend [containment pressure](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentPressure) downward within seconds - if it does not, flow is not really happening.
