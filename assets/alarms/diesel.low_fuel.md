Diesel fuel oil storage at or below 50 gallons remaining - ten percent of the 500 gallon day tank - against the requirement for seven days of continuous rated operation without resupply. The sizing assumption matters: accidents are assumed long, resupply assumed difficult, and the diesel is the plant's only rotating claim to independent power.

Fuel exhaustion converts backup power into a countdown clock, so arrange delivery long before level becomes the constraint. Meanwhile find where the fuel went faster than expected - consumption trending up with no load change suggests a leak, an unauthorized transfer path, or accounting error, each with different urgency.
