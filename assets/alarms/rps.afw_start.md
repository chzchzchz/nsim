Auxiliary feedwater started - low-low level in two steam generators triggered auto-start, or an operator did. This system is the decay-heat backstop: motor-driven and steam-driven pumps across two trains deliver minimum required flow to at least two effective generators within one minute of demand, continuing indefinitely without operator action for the first 30 minutes.

Verification is the whole job in the first minutes: confirm [pumps actually running](AuxiliaryBuilding/EmergencyFeedwaterSystem/SubsystemA/MotorPumpRunning) and [SG levels](ReactorBuilding/SteamGenerator/Water) recovering - design credit assumes discharge valves full-open and no operator help. Only after the assumed 30-minute delay do procedures have anyone trimming flow modulating valves for narrow-band level control.

With AFW feeding and the reactor tripped, the plant sits in stable hot standby for as long as the water lasts - this balance is what the entire EFW design anchors on.
