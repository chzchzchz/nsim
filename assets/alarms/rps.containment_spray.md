Containment spray has actuated. Borated sodium hydroxide solution is being sprayed into upper containment - condensing steam to pull [pressure](ReactorBuilding/ContainmentPressure) down, and scrubbing airborne iodine as droplets carry it to the floor. Two consequences addressed by one engineered system.

Verify delivery end-to-end rather than trusting the actuation: headers [open](AuxiliaryBuilding/ContainmentSpraySystem/SprayHeaderOpen), pumps [running](AuxiliaryBuilding/ContainmentSpraySystem/PumpRunning), and containment pressure actually bending downward within moments of flow. Around 30 minutes of spray additive is stored; when the tank drains to Lo-Lo setpoint, pumps are manually switched to sump recirculation for as long as the accident demands.
