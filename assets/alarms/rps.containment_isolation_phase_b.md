Containment isolation phase B has actuated: the second ring of penetration closures, sealing lines that phase A deliberately left open. Phase B means the containment is being shut as tightly as it can be - the response to confirmed high radiation or pressure inside.

With both phases in, nearly everything crossing the boundary is closed. Confirm post-accident instrumentation remains powered (isolation should never take monitoring dark), and verify no needed flow path - such as [containment spray](rps.containment_spray) suction alignment - was caught in the net.
