Stator coil cooling water flow running low. Cooling is still present but its margin is shrinking; sustained low flow at high load walks conductor temperatures toward insulation life limits measured in months lost per hour abused.

This is an early warning with time to act deliberately: verify pump status and valve lineups, trim real load if temperature trends justify it, and fix the cause before degradation reaches [total flow loss](generator.sccw_no_flow), where the response becomes urgent instead of routine. Watch [real power](TurbineBuilding/Generator/RealPower) against winding temperature indications - the safe operating envelope shrinks as either rises.
